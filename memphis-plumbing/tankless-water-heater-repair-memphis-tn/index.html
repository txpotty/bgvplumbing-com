    What Our Tankless Plumbers Fix in Memphis, TN

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Memphis, TN

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. A breaker that trips under load can mean a shorted element or a loose lug that has been arcing, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Memphis, TN

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Memphis

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Resetting the code without a flush only buys a few days, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, circulate descaling solution through the exchanger for close to an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Memphis

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Well-done installs usually include isolation valves with purge ports for this, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. White vinegar will do in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Memphis

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Memphis

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. We start with the error history in the service menu, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Memphis

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. We check the vent terminal for bird nests and dryer lint, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Memphis, TN

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.
