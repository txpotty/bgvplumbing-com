    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Memphis

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Memphis, TN

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Creeks and rivers rise quickly after a heavy rain, so homes in low areas benefit from backwater valves and good yard drainage. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Memphis

    No pit yet? We find the low spot where water collects, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. New concrete goes back flush and level.

    Sump Pit Installation in Memphis, TN

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Less cycling, longer life.
