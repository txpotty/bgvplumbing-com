    Underground Pipe Repair Options for Memphis, TN Properties

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Memphis

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. One or two holes, not a trench. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, so concrete and established plantings stay put. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Memphis, TN

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. Broken ones leave puddles. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and repair broken runs so runoff gets carried off instead of sitting next to the house. We check the discharge end.

    Underground Storm Drain Repair in Memphis, TN

    Buried storm drains clog with silt, leaves and roots. Creeks and rivers rise quickly after a downpour, so homes in low areas benefit from backwater valves and good yard drainage. Water backs up fast. We clear blockages with a jetter, find cracks and disconnected joints on camera, then fix the broken pipe so water drains to the outlet the way it was built to. Downspout tie-ins get checked.

    Commercial Underground Pipe Repair in Memphis

    Commercial properties run larger and longer buried lines. Losses add up fast. We locate leaks under parking lots, sidewalks and landscaped common areas carefully so concrete cutting stays to the minimum and tenants keep access to the building. We can work nights.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Memphis, TN

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Age does the rest. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, picking the method after the video shows the depth and extent of the damage. All work meets Tennessee plumbing code.

    HOA and Multifamily Underground Repair in Memphis

    HOA common areas and apartment complexes have long buried runs under parking and sidewalks. Access matters. We locate carefully to avoid unnecessary concrete cutting, schedule work around residents, providing photos and pressure test records for managers and owners. We work with your schedule.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Memphis

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene cracks without warning. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. You will notice the pressure.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Memphis

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Landscapers hit them a lot. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, so we repair PVC and poly mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. Vulnerable runs can be moved.
