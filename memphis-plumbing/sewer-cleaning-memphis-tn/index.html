    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Memphis, TN

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Memphis

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Memphis, TN

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Memphis

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Memphis, TN

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Flow slows a little each year. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.
