    Running Toilet Repair in Memphis

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is often caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers quickly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Memphis, TN

    Tough toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt. While some can be cleared with a plunger, deeper blockages may indicate issues further down the drain line or even in the main sewer line. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and sometimes even camera inspections to pinpoint and remove obstructions effectively. We handle everything from foreign objects to excessive toilet paper, ensuring your toilet drains freely again.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Memphis, TN

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We focus on replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Memphis

    A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water annually, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage. Our technicians in Memphis are experts at identifying the source of leaks, whether it's a cracked tank or bowl, a faulty wax ring, or a worn-out fill valve. We use specialized dyes and pressure tests to accurately locate hidden leaks and provide durable repairs, often involving the replacement of seals, gaskets, or the entire flushing mechanism. Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so prompt repair is crucial.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Memphis

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.
