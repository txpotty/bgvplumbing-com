    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Memphis

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Memphis, TN

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Memphis, TN

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. You can see the change on video.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Memphis

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Memphis, TN

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Memphis, TN

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Heavy spring and fall rains can cause flash flooding in Tennessee, and saturated ground often leads to wet crawlspaces and sewer backups. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Memphis

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. On-call trucks are fully equipped. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.
