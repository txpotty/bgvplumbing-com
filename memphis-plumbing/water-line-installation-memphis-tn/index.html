    Water Line Installation Cost Comparison in Memphis, TN

    Run length drives most of the cost, followed by soil and pipe material.     Water Line Pressure Testing and Code Compliance in Memphis, TN

    Every new line gets a hydrostatic pressure test before the trench is closed. The line is capped and pumped above normal working pressure, then watch the gauge for any drop, because a leaking fitting is easy to fix with the pipe exposed and very hard to find once it is buried in Memphis, TN. The gauge does not lie.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Memphis, TN

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. For long runs, HDPE is the tougher of the two, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Memphis, TN. PEX stays covered until burial.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Memphis, TN

    Copper service lines last a long time when the soil and water agree with them. Buried runs get Type K, the thickest wall, joined with flare or compression fittings rated for underground use, and we sleeve the pipe where it passes through the foundation wall so it cannot rub against concrete in your Memphis, TN home. Expect a higher material bill.
