    Long-Lasting Pipe Replacement Services in Memphis, TN

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Memphis

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Memphis, TN

    Many older homes in Memphis, TN still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, fresh water throughout your home.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Memphis

    Beyond residential services, BGV Plumbing also provides commercial pipe replacement for businesses and properties in Memphis. Commercial plumbing systems often face higher demands and require specific knowledge. Whether it's replacing supply lines in an office building, drainage pipes in a restaurant, or addressing complex industrial piping, our experienced commercial plumbers ensure minimal downtime and durable solutions tailored to your business needs.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Memphis, TN

    Deciding between pipe repair and full replacement depends on several factors, including the age of your pipes, the extent of the damage, and the frequency of past issues. A small, isolated leak might be repaired, but widespread corrosion or multiple leaks typically warrant replacement. BGV Plumbing provides honest assessments in Memphis, TN, explaining the pros and cons of each option. We help you make an informed decision that offers the most cost-effective and durable solution for your home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Memphis

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be destructive and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. BGV Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, preventing future issues in the same spot.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Memphis, TN

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace accessible sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Memphis

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. BGV Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Memphis

    If your home in Memphis is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most sensible long-term solution. We can replace all the old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern, durable PEX or copper piping. This upgrades your entire water delivery system, greatly improving water quality and pressure while eliminating future leak concerns from aging infrastructure.
