    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Memphis

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Memphis, TN

    Small drips become floods. Connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and the seams get checked, along with the pan and floor. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Memphis, TN

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Memphis

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Anode Rod Checks in Memphis

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. We pull it and look. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.
