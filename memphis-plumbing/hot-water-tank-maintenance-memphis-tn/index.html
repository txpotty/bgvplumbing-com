    T&P Valve Replacement in Memphis

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.

    Sediment Flushing in Memphis, TN

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. The heat has to push through it to reach the water. Our method is short bursts of line pressure that churn up the bottom. The drain hose runs to a floor drain or outside, never onto a lawn where hot water can burn the grass.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Memphis

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. Those chunks cause the popping. For stubborn scale we use a potable-rated descaler, then flush it clean. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Memphis, TN

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. Swapping a $40 to $80 rod is a lot cheaper than a new tank, and on a tank under eight years old it is usually the best money you can spend on it.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Memphis, TN

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We loosen the hex head with a breaker bar and an impact gun, pull the rod and look at how much core wire is showing. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Memphis

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Memphis, TN

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. A pan under an attic tank is not optional. We check that the pan drain line runs to a safe spot and is not cracked or plugged, because one slow leak over a bedroom can bring down a ceiling. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in Memphis

    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Most of those are quick fixes. Water on the floor is a different story, and the first question we ask is whether it is coming from a fitting on top or from under the jacket. Leaks from under the jacket mean the tank itself has failed.
