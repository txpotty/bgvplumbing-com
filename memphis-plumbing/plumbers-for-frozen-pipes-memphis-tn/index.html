    Pool, Outdoor Equipment and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in Memphis, TN

    Pool equipment lines, outdoor kitchen and ice maker supply tubing and water heater connections in garages are all frequent freeze points.     Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Memphis

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and recommend draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can set that up.

    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in Memphis

    In Memphis, many homes have plumbing lines running through or under the concrete slab, and lines near the edge of the slab or in unheated areas can freeze and burst. If you suspect a slab leak after a freeze, perhaps from a sudden drop in water pressure or unexplained damp spots, our technicians use acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging to precisely locate the leak without destructive guesswork. We then provide focused repair plans to fix the damaged pipe.

    Heat Tape Installation for Exposed Pipes in Memphis

    For pipes in particularly cold or exposed areas, standard insulation might not be enough. BGV Plumbing offers professional heat tape installation, an electric heating cable that warms pipes to prevent freezing. We handle proper installation, including thermostats and ground fault circuit interrupters, for safe and effective operation. This solution is well suited for pipes in crawl spaces, well houses, or along exterior walls in Memphis.

    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Memphis

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawl spaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens, and those spaces get nearly as cold as the outside air at night. Pipes in exterior walls with thin insulation are also at risk. We repair freeze-damaged sections and add proper insulation or reroute lines into conditioned space.     Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Memphis

    The cost for frozen pipe repair in Memphis can range from $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for burst pipe repair requiring extensive access or slab work. Factors include the location of the freeze, whether the pipe has burst, and the type of pipe material. We provide a clear estimate upfront after diagnosis.

    Common signs of a frozen pipe include a sudden drop in water pressure, no water coming from a specific faucet, visible frost on an exposed pipe, or strange gurgling sounds from your drains. If a pipe has burst, you might see water stains, hear dripping, or notice a sharp increase in your water bill.

    If your pipes burst, immediately shut off your main water supply valve to prevent further flooding. Then, call BGV Plumbing at (615) 518-0893 for emergency service. We'll dispatch a plumber to your Memphis location to assess the damage and perform the necessary repairs promptly.

    Ice can split a pipe while frozen, but the leak only appears once the ice melts and water flows again. That is why we pressure test every thawed line. It catches hidden splits before they cause damage.

    A frost-free hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the wall. Brands like Woodford and Prier make dependable models. They greatly reduce freeze damage at outdoor faucets.

    Many policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, though coverage varies. We can provide photos and a written report of the repair. Check with your insurer about your specific policy.

    Minor thawing and simple pipe repairs usually do not require a permit in Memphis. However, if the repair involves significant re-piping, altering the main water line, or work on gas lines tied to water heaters, a plumbing permit may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permitting for you.

    The time it takes to thaw a frozen pipe varies significantly. A small, easily accessible section might thaw in 30 minutes with professional tools. More extensive freezes, especially in hidden walls or under floors, can take several hours. Our goal is always to restore water flow as quickly and safely as possible.
