    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Memphis, TN

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, and a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over a few years than repeated repairs and drywall patches. We price both.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Memphis, TN

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. Look for white crust. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, then clean, reflux and resolder the joint, or replace the fitting entirely with a new sweat or press fitting. We test before closing up.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Repair in Memphis

    Many commercial buildings use larger diameter copper for domestic water supply. Leaks affect more than one unit. Leaks in these systems require careful shutdown planning, so we repair commercial copper lines with press fittings to reduce downtime and avoid open flame near occupied spaces. We can add shutoffs for next time.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Memphis

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. The meter gives it away. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. We test before backfill.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Memphis, TN

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most vulnerable during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Memphis

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, so we locate them with a thermal camera and moisture meter before cutting a single opening in the wall or ceiling. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Memphis

Early or late work is available.
