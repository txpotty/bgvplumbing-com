    Sewer Cleaning in Memphis, TN

    A blocked main stops every drain. We clear the main through the cleanout with a large cable machine and a root-cutting head, then follow with a jetter if the line has heavy grease or root buildup. Then we test.

    Main Sewer Line Cleaning in Memphis, TN

    A clogged main shows up as backups at the lowest drains first. Access starts at the cleanout. A large cable machine with a root-cutting head clears most main line blockages, and when roots or grease are heavy, we follow up with a jetter to scrub the pipe walls clean. Then we camera it.

    High-Pressure Hydro Jetting in Memphis

    A hydro jetter sends water through a nozzle at several thousand psi. The spray cleans the whole pipe wall. Grease, scale, sludge and fine roots break loose and wash downstream, which a cable alone cannot do. Good pipes only.

    Sewer Line Repair Options in Memphis

    Spot repairs work in some cases. A single cracked or offset joint can often be fixed by digging one hole and replacing that section, which costs less long term. The camera shows which.

    Storm Drain Cleaning in Memphis, TN

    Gutters, downspouts and yard drains move a lot of water. A clogged yard drain sends water to the foundation. We clear the lines with a cable or jetter and flush them to make sure they carry water away from the house and toward the street or a dry well, before the next storm. Creeks and rivers rise quickly after a heavy rain, so homes in low areas benefit from backwater valves and good yard drainage.

    Sewer Cleaning and Hydro Jetting in Memphis, TN

    Main lines with heavy grease or root buildup respond best to jetting. Snaking opens the line. Jetting scrubs the full pipe wall and flushes debris to the city main, leaving the line clean enough for a clear camera inspection afterward. Two steps, one visit.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Memphis

    Maintenance costs less than emergencies. Regular cleaning prevents backups. Homes with big trees near the sewer and businesses with busy kitchens often benefit from cleaning every one to two years, so the line never gets close to a full blockage. Ask about a schedule.
