    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Memphis, TN

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Memphis, TN

    Older homes in Memphis often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. Then we locate it. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Memphis

    Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have fairly hard water. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Memphis

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Tennessee houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. We test each one directly. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Memphis, TN

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Memphis, TN

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. We mark problem spots from above. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more common in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Memphis

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Memphis

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Memphis, TN

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.
