    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes.     Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Memphis, TN

    Whether your home in Memphis, TN has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both.     Slab Leak Reroute in Memphis

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Memphis, TN

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel.     Spot Repair Through the Slab in Memphis, TN
