    Leak Detection Cost Comparison in Memphis, TN

    Professional leak detection typically costs $150 to $450 for a standard home, depending on the system size and how many areas need testing. Pool leak detection usually runs $250 to $500. Repair costs vary a lot, from $150 for a fixture fix to several thousand dollars for a slab reroute. Accurate detection keeps the repair cost as low as it can be.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Memphis, TN

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to extensive drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to precisely pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's interior.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Memphis

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, oddly green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, targeted hole.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Memphis, TN

    Leaks in underground water lines, often leading from the meter to your home, can result in significant water loss and property damage. We employ ground microphones and tracer gas detection to locate these buried leaks precisely. Factors like tree roots, ground shifting and corrosion in older galvanized pipes can contribute to these issues, making professional detection important.

    Slab Leak Detection in Memphis

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use specialized acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you significant repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines beneath the slab.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Memphis, TN

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in most yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills jump in summer.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Memphis, TN

    Sewer line leaks can cause unpleasant odors, slow drains and potential health hazards. Our team uses camera inspections to visually identify breaks, cracks or blockages within your sewer lines. This method allows for accurate location of the leak without guesswork, leading to more efficient and targeted repairs. We address sewer leaks in all types of piping materials common in Memphis homes, from PVC to cast iron.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Memphis

    Summer heat and wind can evaporate a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak protects the pool shell and the soil around it.
