    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Memphis, TN

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Memphis

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Gas work is done to code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Memphis

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Water Softener Service in Memphis

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. Hardness gets tested after. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Memphis, TN

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Memphis, TN

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. Under-sink checks, toilet valve tests, fixture runs, washer hose inspection and a pressure reading cover the house. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Larger repairs get a written price.

    Repipe Assessments in Memphis, TN

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. We identify the pipe type, look for signs of failure and tell you whether repiping is urgent or can wait. We quote PEX and copper. Work can be phased.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Memphis, TN

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. The recording is yours. If cleaning or repair makes sense, we explain the choices.

    Softener Upkeep in Memphis, TN

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. We check the bypass and drain line. We measure hardness after.
