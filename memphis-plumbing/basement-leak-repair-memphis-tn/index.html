    Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Memphis

    A burst pipe or a failed sump during a storm can put inches of water on the floor in minutes. Call us and shut off the main water valve if you can do so safely.     Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Memphis

    A dependable sump pump is crucial for preventing basement flooding, particularly in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall. We offer expert installation of high-capacity sump pumps, including battery backup systems, to ensure continuous protection. Our technicians also provide prompt repair and maintenance services for existing sump pumps, ensuring they operate effectively when you need them most.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Memphis

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm typically points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced without trouble.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Memphis, TN

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks often show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then confirm the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Memphis

    When exterior excavation isn't feasible, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less disruptive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Memphis, TN

    For complete basement leak prevention, exterior waterproofing involves excavating around your foundation and applying waterproof membranes or coatings. This creates a strong barrier against moisture intrusion from the outside. While more involved, this method offers superior long-term protection against water damage, especially for older homes in Memphis.
