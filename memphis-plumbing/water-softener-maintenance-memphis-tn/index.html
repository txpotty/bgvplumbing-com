    What Our Water Softener Maintenance Covers in Memphis, TN

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Memphis

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Memphis

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Memphis, TN

    Plans take the remembering off your plate. You get a reminder call before each yearly visit, and if the control valve fails between visits you get priority booking. Repairs found during a plan visit come at a lower rate than a regular call.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Memphis, TN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Memphis

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle usually point to worn seals and spacers. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Memphis

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. Plan on new media every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Memphis

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Memphis, TN

    Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Morton and the store brands all use different valves, and parts for some of them only come through the dealer. Seal kits for the common valves ride on the truck. For a proprietary unit, we tell you up front if a part has to be ordered and how long it usually takes.
