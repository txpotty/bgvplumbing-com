    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Hendersonville

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not.     Brands We Match in Hendersonville

    American Standard, Kohler, TOTO and Gerber sell bowls separately for many two-piece models, but the tank bolts, gasket style and bolt spacing have to match exactly. A near match will leak. We confirm the pairing from the model number before ordering, which avoids a second trip.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Hendersonville, TN

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old pipe cracks easily.     Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Hendersonville

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. We look closely before quoting.     Changing Toilet Bowl Styles in Hendersonville
