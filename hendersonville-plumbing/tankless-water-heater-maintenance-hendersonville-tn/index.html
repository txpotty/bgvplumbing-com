    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Hendersonville, TN

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations.     Same-Day Tankless Help in Hendersonville, TN

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. Emergency calls get common parts and a descaling kit, and most heaters run again before we leave.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Hendersonville

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Hendersonville, TN

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Hendersonville, TN

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. We take a flow and temperature reading on each heater. Then we compare them.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Hendersonville, TN

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. Future flushes then take minutes to set up.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Hendersonville

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.
