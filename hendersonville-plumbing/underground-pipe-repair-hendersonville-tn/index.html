    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Hendersonville

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. Full main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on depth.

    For underground repairs, we usually use durable materials like PVC, HDPE for trenchless methods or occasionally copper for certain water lines. These materials resist corrosion, root intrusion and ground shifts, providing a long-lasting solution that meets Tennessee plumbing standards.

    Common signs include a water bill that jumps without explanation, a meter that moves with all water off, low pressure and wet or sinking spots in the yard. A hissing sound near the meter box can also signal a leak.

    The length of an underground pipe repair depends heavily on the severity and location of the damage, as well as the repair method chosen. Minor repairs using trenchless techniques might be completed in a single day. More extensive issues, such as full sewer line replacements requiring excavation, could take two to five days, including restoration time.

    BGV Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the right repair techniques for each material.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection promptly.

    Trenchless pipe repair offers several key advantages, including minimal disruption to your yard, landscaping and concrete, which can save on restoration costs. It's often faster than traditional excavation and less invasive, preserving the look and value of your property. This method is also durable, frequently extending the lifespan of your pipes.
