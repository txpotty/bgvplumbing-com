    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Hendersonville

    A single clogged sink or tub in Hendersonville, TN usually runs $200 to $350. Root-filled mains and jobs that need jetting usually fall in the $400 to $1,000 range. Location changes the price. An easy outdoor cleanout keeps costs lower, and pulling a toilet or going through a roof vent adds labor. You get the number before we start.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Hendersonville's older homes take the most damage. They also make our job riskier. Let us know if you used a chemical, because that water splashes when the trap comes off.

    Not when the right cable and cutter are used at the right speed. Any cleaning tool can make cracked, crushed or rotted pipe worse. A camera check prevents surprises. On an older line with a history of trouble, we often look first so we know what we are cutting through.

    Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. Do not wait on that one. Several fixtures acting up at once almost always points to a main line clog rather than a single trap under one sink.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. A jetter scours the walls with water at high pressure. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.

    Clay or older cast iron lines with trees nearby usually need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera look can set the timing. How quickly roots return after the last cut tells us if yearly or every-other-year service fits your line.

    Plan on 1 to 3 hours for a single clogged drain. A root-choked main or a long jetting job may take 3 to 6 hours. You get a time estimate first. After looking at the cleanout and hearing which drains are slow, the tech can narrow the estimate.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. A cable can kink and wrap a hand or wrist fast. They also get stuck. A rental cable stuck in an old clay line can turn a $300 rooter call into a dig to retrieve it.
