    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Hendersonville

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works.     Tankless Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville, TN

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville, TN

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. We know those manuals well.     Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville, TN

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Hendersonville

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.
