    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Hendersonville

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Hendersonville

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep everyone out of the area and shut off water use. We find the blockage and get flow moving, sometimes with a jetter and sometimes with a pump truck. Cleanup guidance comes with it.

    Septic Tee Repair in Hendersonville

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Hendersonville

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.
