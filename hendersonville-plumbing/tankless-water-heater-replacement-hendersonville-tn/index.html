    Picking a Tankless for Your Hendersonville, TN Home

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Hendersonville

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hendersonville

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Hendersonville

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hendersonville

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Hendersonville

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Humidity promotes condensation and corrosion on cold water lines and fittings in crawlspaces, which is worth checking during routine service. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Navien Venting and Install in Hendersonville

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Warranty depends on it.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Hendersonville, TN

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. We test both.
