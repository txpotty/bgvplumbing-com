    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Hendersonville

    Slab leaks, where pipes under your concrete foundation leak, are a serious concern for homeowners in Hendersonville. These leaks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and wasted water. BGV Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to pinpoint the exact location of the leak. Depending on the severity and accessibility, we offer targeted pipe repair or rerouting and replacement solutions to fully resolve the issue, protecting your home's structural integrity.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Hendersonville

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Many older homes in Hendersonville, TN still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to rust and corrosion on the inside. This leads to reduced water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. BGV Plumbing provides complete galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also protects your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. BGV Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Hendersonville, TN. Our plumbers are skilled in the careful soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up year after year.

    Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. Cold snaps can lead to burst pipes, especially in exposed areas or unheated spaces. When a pipe bursts in your Hendersonville, TN home, it causes immediate water damage and requires urgent attention. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences. We work quickly to minimize disruption and water loss.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    PEX Repiping in Hendersonville

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.
