    Waterless Urinal Installation in Hendersonville

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Hendersonville

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. Busy bars can see hundreds of flushes a night, so we install valves that recover fast and carriers that stay tight. We can fit tamper-resistant valves. Night installs keep you open.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Hendersonville

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Hendersonville

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. We set up a schedule with you. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Hendersonville, TN

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Hendersonville, TN

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. We run the drain to the nearest stack or building drain, add a vent or an air admittance valve where local code allows one, and pick a compact bowl that fits the space without a full commercial carrier. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Hendersonville, TN

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Your city may want a permit. We check that first.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Hendersonville

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. The old flush valve comes off, the supply gets capped behind the wall, and the new waterless bowl goes on the same carrier with its cartridge housing tied into the existing drain. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.
