    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Hendersonville

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper.     Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Disc Faucet Repair in Hendersonville

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap.     Repair means replacing worn parts inside the faucet you have, like washers, seats, O-rings or a cartridge, while replacement means a whole new faucet on the sink. Repair is usually cheaper. Replacement makes sense for cracked or discontinued faucets.

    One to two hours at the most for a standard repair with common parts. Specialty parts can add a second visit. One visit is the goal, and the truck carries enough stock that most repairs never need a second trip to the supply house.
