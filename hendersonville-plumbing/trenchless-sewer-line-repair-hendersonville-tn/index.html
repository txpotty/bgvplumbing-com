    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Hendersonville, TN

    Sewer lines in Hendersonville, TN can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more frequent in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Hendersonville, TN

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Tennessee homeowners.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Hendersonville

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Hendersonville homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
