    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Hendersonville, TN

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    For sewer line replacements, we primarily use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your needs and local soil conditions.

    Roots typically enter through existing gaps at joints, then expand as they grow. Over time they can crack clay pipe, separate joints and create a full blockage. Cutting roots helps short-term, but sealing or replacing the joint is the lasting fix.

    Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smells in the yard and wet or unusually green patches over the line. Water backing up into a tub or shower when you run the washing machine is another common warning sign.
