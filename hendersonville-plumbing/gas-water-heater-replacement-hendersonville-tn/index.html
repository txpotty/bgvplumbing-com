    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Hendersonville

    The 40 and 50 gallon gas tank is the workhorse of most Tennessee homes. When yours starts leaking from the bottom seam or produces rusty water, replacement is the only real fix. We match the new tank's height and diameter to your space and reuse the venting when it is in good shape. Newer tanks are sometimes taller due to efficiency standards, so we check clearance before ordering. Most of these jobs take two to three hours.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Hendersonville

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Hendersonville, TN

    For homes in Hendersonville, TN where traditional venting is not possible, or for those seeking higher efficiency, power vent gas water heaters are an excellent choice. These units use a powered fan to exhaust combustion gases, offering more flexibility in installation location. Our technicians are experienced in installing these more complex systems. That includes running new PVC vent piping and making proper electrical connections for the blower motor, all while following local codes.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Hendersonville

    Power vent gas water heaters use an electric blower to push exhaust through PVC pipe, which lets them sit far from a chimney or roof flue. When the blower motor or pressure switch fails again and again, replacing the whole unit can be the better choice. We install new power vent models and confirm a dedicated outlet is available. Venting length and number of elbows must stay within the manufacturer's limits. Power vent tanks cost more but solve tricky placement problems.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Hendersonville

    Atmospheric Vent Tank Upgrades in Hendersonville, TN

    Most older homes have atmospheric vent water heaters that rely on natural draft up a metal flue. We inspect the flue pipe for rust, proper slope and correct connector size during replacement. A loose or undersized vent can spill exhaust into the garage or closet. We replace damaged vent sections and draft hoods as needed. Then we test draft with the burner running before leaving.
