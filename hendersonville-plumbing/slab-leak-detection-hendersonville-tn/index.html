    Accurate Slab Leak Detection Services in Hendersonville, TN

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Hendersonville

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the faint sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Hendersonville home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Hendersonville, TN

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Hendersonville.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Hendersonville

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. This step-by-step approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Hendersonville, TN.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Hendersonville

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.
