    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hendersonville, TN

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Hendersonville, TN homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.
