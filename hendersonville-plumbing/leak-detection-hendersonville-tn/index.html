    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Hendersonville, TN

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it.     Preventive Leak Inspections in Hendersonville, TN

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves.
