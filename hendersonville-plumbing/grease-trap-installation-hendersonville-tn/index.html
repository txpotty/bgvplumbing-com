    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Hendersonville, TN

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. The unit goes on the floor or in a shallow recess, and the flow control fitting goes upstream. Clearance matters most. We leave room to pull the lid and baffle without unhooking the sink, because a trap nobody can open is a trap nobody cleans.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Hendersonville, TN

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Hendersonville, TN

    Price follows size and location more than brand. A small indoor trap set on an open floor near the sink is the cheapest version of this job. Outdoor tanks with digging and a permit sit at the top of the range. We quote after a site visit.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in Hendersonville, TN

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. Fail that and you can face fines or a surcharge on your sewer bill. We install to pass. We add a sampling port if they require it and size to the authority's own formula.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Hendersonville, TN

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. Long runs to the trap let grease cool and coat the line, so we keep the trap as close to the fixtures as we can. Closer is better. For outdoor tanks we measure the hose run from where a pump truck can park.

    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Hendersonville, TN

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, though some do. We see them in licensed home kitchens and in houses where the sewer lateral keeps clogging with grease. A small 4 to 7 GPM unit under the kitchen sink catches most of it, and cleaning takes about ten minutes with a spatula and a bucket. Check with your local health office first.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Hendersonville, TN

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more. The work is excavation and a level gravel bed, then the tank and its inlet and outlet piping. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. We plan the dig and the backfill around what the soil report and the open hole actually show us. Risers bring the lids to grade.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Hendersonville

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. The purchase price is higher than a passive trap. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.
