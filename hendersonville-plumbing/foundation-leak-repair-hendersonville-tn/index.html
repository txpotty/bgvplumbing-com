    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Hendersonville, TN

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix. A small opening gets cut, the pipe is exposed and repaired, the line is tested, and the hole is backfilled in layers and patched level. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Hendersonville, TN

    Older homes in Hendersonville, TN often have soft copper running under the slab.     Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Hendersonville, TN
