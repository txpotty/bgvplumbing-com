    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Jackson

    When water enters through foundation walls or the slab joint, the cause is usually surface water pooling against the house. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. We seal penetrations where pipes pass through the wall and can install interior drain channels that carry seepage to a sump.     Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Jackson, TN

    Heavy rain can lead to significant hydrostatic pressure against basement walls, forcing water through concrete. Heavy spring and fall rains can cause flash flooding in Tennessee, and saturated ground often leads to wet crawlspaces and sewer backups. BGV Plumbing installs interior and exterior drainage systems, including French drains and weeping tile systems, to relieve this pressure. We also implement sump pump systems to efficiently remove collected water, keeping your basement dry.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Jackson, TN

    For comprehensive basement leak prevention, exterior waterproofing involves excavating around your foundation and applying waterproof membranes or coatings.     Supply Line Leak Repair in Jackson

    Water lines that run along basement ceilings and walls can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Water chemistry affects how fast copper corrodes, and older galvanized lines frequently rust from the inside. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section with copper or PEX and pressure test the repair. If the line shows widespread wear, we will tell you whether a partial repipe makes more sense.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Jackson

    A reliable sump pump is crucial for preventing basement flooding, particularly in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall.     Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Jackson

    Interior or exterior drain tile systems can become clogged with sediment or tree roots over time, leaving them useless.     Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Jackson, TN

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool quickly.
