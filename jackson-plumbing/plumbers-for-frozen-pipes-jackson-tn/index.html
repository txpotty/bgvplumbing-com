    While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, materials like PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, are more flexible and resistant to bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC.     Tennessee adopts the International Plumbing Code at the state level, and many local jurisdictions enforce their own adopted edition with local amendments.     While any pipe can freeze, older galvanized steel or copper pipes in uninsulated areas are often more susceptible to bursting due to their rigid material.
