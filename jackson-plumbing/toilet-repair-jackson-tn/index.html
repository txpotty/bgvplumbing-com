    Commercial Toilet Repair in Jackson

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Jackson

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. We clean it with a scrub pad, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. The float gets set so the water level sits roughly one inch under the top of the overflow tube.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Jackson

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. The tank gets noticeably quieter.
