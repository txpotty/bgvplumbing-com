    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Jackson

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the fastest fix. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Jackson, TN

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is popular in long single-story ranch homes.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Jackson

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Rapid swings between mild and freezing weather in Jackson can stress joints and fittings over time as pipes expand and contract. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Jackson, TN

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
