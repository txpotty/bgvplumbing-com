    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Jackson

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. It shows up as green crust. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, and inside the wall the first sign is usually a pinhole or a weeping joint that stains the drywall below it.
