    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Jackson, TN: The Details

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Jackson, TN

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Jackson, TN

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Jackson, TN

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Composite Sink Installation in Jackson

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Jackson, TN

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Jackson

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate chips if you pry. The new sink needs to cover the old cutout fully.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Jackson

    Drop-in sinks are common in Tennessee homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Jackson

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can flood a cabinet within hours. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. We carry common sizes. If you have a standard 33 by 22 inch drop-in, we can usually remove the damaged sink and set a new one that day.
