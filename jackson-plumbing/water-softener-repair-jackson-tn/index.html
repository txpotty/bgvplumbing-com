    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Jackson

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Jackson, TN

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair usually wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Jackson

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Control Valve Repair in Jackson

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. BGV Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, restoring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed your repair in Jackson.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Jackson, TN

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Jackson, TN

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, allowing a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Jackson, TN

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. BGV Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these unique units in Jackson, TN, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Jackson

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the cause. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.
