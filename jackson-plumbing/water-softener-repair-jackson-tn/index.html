    Water Softener Problems We Fix in Jackson, TN

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Jackson

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Jackson

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Jackson, TN

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol valves ride on the truck, so most rebuilds happen on the first visit.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Jackson

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Jackson, TN

    Drips around fittings waste water. We trace it to the seal, O-ring, hose or internal part that failed. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Jackson, TN home and test it under line pressure.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Jackson, TN

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Jackson

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores. Each has its own valve design and common failures. BGV Plumbing technicians know those designs, so diagnosis is quick. Make or model, the repair in your Jackson garage is done right.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Jackson, TN

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.
