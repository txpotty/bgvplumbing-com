    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Jackson

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. The tech pulls the pilot assembly and checks the thermocouple tip, the orifice and the igniter wire. A thermocouple that tests weak on the millivolt meter will not hold the gas valve's magnet open, so the pilot drops out a minute after you relight it by the label. Common pilot kits ride on most of our trucks.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Jackson, TN

    Scalding water at the kitchen tap is a valve problem until proven otherwise. Some controls drift high as the sensor ages, and some dials get turned past hot. Our plumbers in Jackson, TN measure the outlet temperature at the nearest fixture, compare it to the dial and replace the gas control if it is overshooting, because water at 140 degrees can burn a child's skin in a few seconds. A setting near 120 degrees works for most households.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Jackson, TN

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs usually run $150 to $350. A gas control valve replacement tends to land between $350 and $700.     Tank Leaks and Anode Rods in Jackson

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We pull the anode, check how much magnesium or aluminum is left and replace it if the core wire is showing. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Jackson home that has not started leaking from the shell. A wet bottom seam is different. That tank needs replacing.

    Burner Assembly and Venting Repair in Jackson

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. We pull the burner, clear the ports and look at the flame arrestor screen underneath. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Jackson home. A smoke pencil shows the draft.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Jackson, TN

    Atmospheric tanks rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue with an upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot. A loose, rusted or sagging section lets exhaust spill back into the house. We fix the vent. Each joint gets three screws, the connector gets proper clearance from wood and drywall, and we run the heater for several minutes with a smoke test at the draft hood to watch the exhaust go up. Spillage gets fixed before we leave.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Repair in Jackson, TN

    Restaurants and salons in Jackson, TN burn through hot water faster than any house, and a down heater stops the business. Most commercial tanks hold 75 to 100 gallons and run far higher BTU inputs than a home unit.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Jackson

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Then each part gets a meter test.
