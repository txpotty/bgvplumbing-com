    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Jackson

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Jackson, TN

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Jackson.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Jackson

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Tennessee homes.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Jackson, TN

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.
