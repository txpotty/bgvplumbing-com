    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Jackson, TN

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do.     Rental and Hotel Shower Replacement in Jackson

    Rental showers need to be durable and easy to fix. We use commercial parts and the same cartridge in every unit, which keeps repairs fast. Work fits tenant schedules. Between tenants is ideal.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Jackson, TN

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. Demo goes to the studs, damaged wood gets replaced and a new waterproofed pan goes in with the valve set to tile depth. Slope matters most. We flood-test before tile.

    Custom Shower Replacement in Jackson, TN

    Built-in benches and niches change the plumbing plan. Benches move the ideal valve and handheld spots, and niches must avoid the supply lines, so we plan together before framing. More outlets need larger supplies. We size those to your water pressure.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Jackson

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low. It makes a real difference.

    Accessible Shower Replacement in Jackson, TN

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. The valve goes within reach of a seat, blocking goes in for grab bars and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Entries stay wide. We plan for a seat.
