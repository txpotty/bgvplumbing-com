    Water Softener Service in Morristown

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Morristown

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. We repair failures. Weather protection gets checked too. Late tests can mean fines.

    Softener Upkeep in Morristown, TN

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. We check the bypass and drain line. We measure hardness after.
