    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Morristown

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Morristown, TN

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Morristown, TN

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Drain Line Repiping in Morristown

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Morristown

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Morristown, TN

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Morristown

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.
