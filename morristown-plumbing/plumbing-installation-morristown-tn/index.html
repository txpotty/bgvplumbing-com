    Water Heater Installation in Morristown

    Tank or tankless, gas or electric, the first question is how many people shower in the morning. We size the heater to that peak demand and your fuel source, then check the venting and gas supply before quoting. Gas heaters need the closest look. A draft hood that spills or a flue with the wrong pitch can push carbon monoxide back into the house. We haul the old tank away before we leave.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Morristown, TN

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. An ejector pump in a sealed basin lifts it up to the main drain. Pump and basin are sized to the fixtures, the basin is vented to code, and the discharge gets a check valve and a shutoff so the pump can come out for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    Sump Pump Installation in Morristown, TN

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. We run the discharge line far enough from the foundation that the water does not cycle straight back into the pit.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Morristown, TN

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. On a whole-house repipe that means fewer joints hidden behind drywall and fewer places for a slow leak to start rotting a stud. It can also stretch when water freezes. Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. Stretch or not, we still insulate runs in exterior walls and crawl spaces.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Morristown, TN

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Water Heater Replacement in Morristown

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. We install 40 and 50 gallon tanks, plus 75 gallon units for big households, from Rheem and Bradford White, with new flex connectors, a relief line piped to code and a vent check on every job. We also check whether you need an expansion tank. One visit covers most installs.

    Softener Loop and Conditioner Installs in Morristown

    Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. Some homes already have a softener loop plumbed in, usually in the garage, which turns the install into a two-hour job. No loop means we cut into the main line and add one. Figure on a few extra hours for that. A salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits the same loop.

    PEX Repiping in Morristown

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. It also handles a freeze better. Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.
