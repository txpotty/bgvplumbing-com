    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Morristown, TN

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company.     Residential Gas Leak Detection in Morristown

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements.     Appliance Connector Replacement in Morristown

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. If your range or water heater still has one, it should come out. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Morristown

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. After an 811 locate, we excavate, cut out the failed run and lay polyethylene with a tracer wire beside it. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Morristown trench rules.

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time.
