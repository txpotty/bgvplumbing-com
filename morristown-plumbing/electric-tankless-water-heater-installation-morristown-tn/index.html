    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Morristown

    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit, and we install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.
