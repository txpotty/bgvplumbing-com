    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Morristown, TN

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Morristown

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Morristown

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Morristown, TN

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Morristown

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Morristown, TN

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Morristown, TN

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Morristown

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.
