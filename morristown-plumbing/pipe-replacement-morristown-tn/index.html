    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Morristown, TN

    PEX Pipe Installation in Morristown

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have fairly hard water. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Morristown

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. Finding the exact spot comes first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Morristown, TN

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Morristown, TN

    Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Morristown, TN

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Morristown

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Morristown, TN

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    PEX Repiping in Morristown

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.
