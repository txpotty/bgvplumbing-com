    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Morristown

    The cost of toilet replacement in Morristown, TN typically ranges from $400 to $800, including the new toilet fixture and labor. This can vary based on the toilet model you choose, any necessary repairs to the flange or subfloor, and the difficulty of the installation.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location usually does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    We recommend dependable brands known for their quality, efficiency, and availability of parts. Popular choices include Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, and Gerber. Each offers a range of styles and features, from basic models to advanced low-flow or comfort height options. We can help you select the best fit for your needs.

    We often recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their long service life.

    Manufacturers typically warranty parts for 1 to 5 years and porcelain longer. We guarantee our workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by the installation.

    The rough-in measurement refers to the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe (the bolts that secure the toilet). Standard rough-ins are usually 12 inches, but 10-inch and 14-inch options exist. Knowing your rough-in is essential to make sure your new toilet fits correctly in the existing space.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a typical household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    Most standard toilet replacements can be completed by a professional plumber in about 2 to 4 hours. This includes the removal of the old unit, preparation of the flange, installation of the new toilet, and testing for leaks. More complex installations might take a bit longer.
