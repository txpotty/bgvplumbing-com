    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Morristown, TN

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner.     Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Morristown, TN

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Morristown

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Morristown, TN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Morristown

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Drainage System Integration in Morristown, TN

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Morristown

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Morristown

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains. The size of the wall, how wet it gets, which method fits and how hard the wall is to get to all move the number. Access matters most. Nothing starts until you have the quote in writing.

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Poured concrete takes crystalline coatings well, while block often needs an elastomeric membrane or a drainage board.

    It removes the water mold needs, so new growth slows down. It does not kill mold that is already there. Any mold already growing needs to be remediated before or alongside the waterproofing, and once the space is dry and sealed it tends to stay that way.

    Bubbling paint is a big one. Soft drywall near the floor, stained baseboards, a musty smell and white crust on block or stucco all point to moisture, and in a shower, loose tiles or cracked grout tend to mean water has already gotten behind the wall. A moisture meter confirms it in minutes.

    Yes. Our installs carry a written warranty on labor and materials, and the length depends on the system we put in, while many membranes and injection resins also come with a manufacturer warranty. The details are spelled out in your service agreement.

    Look low on the wall first. Damp patches, a musty smell, mold on the bottom of the drywall, white powdery efflorescence on block or brick, and puddles after rain all point to water coming through. Stair-step cracks and bowing walls also deserve a closer look.

    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before any sealer goes on, and for larger growth, generally more than about ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. We remove smaller affected areas ourselves.

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out. Exterior waterproofing stops water before it reaches the wall, which means digging down to the footing and applying a membrane to the outside face. Outside costs more.
