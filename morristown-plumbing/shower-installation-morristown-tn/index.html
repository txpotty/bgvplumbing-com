    Shower Installation Work We Do in Morristown, TN

    Shower Pan Installation in Morristown

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Morristown

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Digital Shower Systems in Morristown, TN

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Morristown, TN

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. We install commercial valves with tamper-resistant trim, vandal-resistant heads and drains sized for several showers running at once, all to commercial code. We work around your business. Thermostatic mixing at the source can protect every head in the room.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Morristown, TN

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Morristown, TN

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Morristown

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. A gauge reading comes first.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Morristown, TN

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. The quote covers everything up front.
