    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Morristown, TN

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Morristown, TN

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are crucial for separating FOG from wastewater. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Morristown, TN, ensuring your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.
