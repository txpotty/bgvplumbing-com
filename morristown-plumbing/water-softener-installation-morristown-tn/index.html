    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Morristown

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Morristown, TN

    Building a home in Morristown, TN? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Morristown, TN

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.

    Softener Loop Installation in Morristown

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Morristown, TN

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Replacement in Morristown, TN

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Morristown

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.
