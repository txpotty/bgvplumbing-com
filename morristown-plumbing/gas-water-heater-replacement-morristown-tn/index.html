    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Morristown

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Morristown, TN

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Morristown, TN
