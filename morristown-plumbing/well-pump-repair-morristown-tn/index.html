    Jet Pump Repair in Morristown, TN

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Morristown is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating efficiently again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Morristown

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Morristown, TN

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Morristown, TN

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.
