    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Morristown

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can promptly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge.     Low Shower Pressure Repair in Morristown, TN

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed quickly.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Morristown

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are experienced at diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Morristown, TN

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are usually caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Morristown

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are simple to clean.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Morristown

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort.     Low water pressure can be caused by mineral buildup in the shower head or pipes, a faulty pressure-balancing valve, a partially closed main water valve, or even issues with your home's water heater. Our plumbers can diagnose the exact cause.

    A stuck or worn pressure-balance spool often causes temperature swings when other fixtures are used. Replacing the cartridge or valve typically solves it.

    Key indicators include a dripping faucet, low water pressure, inconsistent water temperature, standing water in the shower, strange noises from the pipes, or visible leaks around the shower base. Addressing these signs early can prevent more serious damage.

    Most common shower repairs, such as fixing a leaky faucet or clearing a drain, can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, like replacing a shower valve, might take half a day. We aim for efficient service to minimize inconvenience.

    Leaks behind the wall are often due to a faulty shower valve, loose pipe connections, or cracks in the shower pan or tile grout.
