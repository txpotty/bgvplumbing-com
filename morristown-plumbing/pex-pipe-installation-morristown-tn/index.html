    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Morristown, TN

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Morristown

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.
