    Electric Element Service in Morristown, TN

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Morristown, TN

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Morristown

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Loose vents leak CO. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Anode Rod Checks in Morristown

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Morristown
