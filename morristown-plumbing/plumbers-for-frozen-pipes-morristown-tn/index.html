    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Morristown

    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe in Morristown usually costs $150 to $400, and burst pipe repair typically runs $300 to $1,000. Freeze-proofing is cheaper. Insulating a crawlspace, adding heat cable to the worst run, and swapping outdoor faucets for frost-free models usually costs a few hundred dollars, which is less than a single burst pipe repair.

    Insulate exposed pipes in attics, crawlspaces and garages, and seal air leaks around them. Pull hoses off outdoor faucets in fall. On the coldest nights, let a faucet on an outside wall drip, open the cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, and keep the thermostat at the same setting overnight instead of turning it down.

    Pipe location matters more than brand. Metal pipe cracks more easily. Metal pipe in cold spots splits most, and PEX often survives except at the fittings.

    Keep cold air away from the pipe. Seal and insulate around exposed lines. Close crawlspace vents for the season, insulate attic and garage runs, swap old hose bibs for frost-free models, and add self-regulating heat cable on any run that still gets too cold despite insulation.

    You can open the faucet and gently warm an exposed pipe with a hair dryer. Never use a torch. If the frozen section is inside a wall, a ceiling or under the floor, call a plumber instead, because you cannot see whether it has already split, and thawing it can release water into the structure.

    Check which fixtures work. The frozen section sits between the last working fixture and the first dead one. A dead sink on an outside wall usually means ice in that wall, which a thermal camera can confirm.

    No pipe is freeze-proof. PEX tolerates freezing better than most. It stretches instead of splitting, but its fittings can still fail, so placement and insulation matter most.

    Most thawing jobs take one to two hours. Burst repairs take one to four hours. Repairs inside finished ceilings or walls take longer because we open the drywall, repair the pipe, test it and leave the opening clean and ready for a patch, and multiple splits on one run add time as well.
