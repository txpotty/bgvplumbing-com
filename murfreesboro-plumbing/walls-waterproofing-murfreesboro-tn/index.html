    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Murfreesboro, TN

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Murfreesboro, TN

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Murfreesboro

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Murfreesboro, TN

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Hot, humid summers and cool winters with frequent freeze and thaw cycles are the main climate stresses on plumbing in Tennessee. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Murfreesboro, TN basement.

    Drainage System Integration in Murfreesboro, TN

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Murfreesboro, TN properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Murfreesboro, TN

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Murfreesboro

    Many homes in Murfreesboro have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Murfreesboro

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can crack buried pipes over time. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Murfreesboro

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Murfreesboro, TN can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump usually fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide transparent, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Look for signs such as damp or wet spots on basement or crawl space walls, a musty odor, visible mold or mildew growth, efflorescence, a white powdery residue, on concrete or brick, and standing water. Cracks in foundation walls or bowing walls also point to potential water entry issues. If you notice any of these, it's wise to get a professional assessment.

    Shower remodels that change plumbing or drain locations often require a permit. Simple membrane replacement may not. We check local requirements and obtain permits when needed.

    We use a range of high-performance waterproofing membranes and coatings depending on the application and foundation type. These can include liquid asphalt-modified polyurethane, rubberized asphalt membranes, bentonite clay systems, and crystalline coatings. Brands often include well-known names known for their durability and effectiveness in preventing water penetration.

    A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Murfreesboro may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. BGV Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty valid.
