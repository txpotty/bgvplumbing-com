    Sink Parts Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Murfreesboro

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Murfreesboro

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Murfreesboro

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Murfreesboro

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Murfreesboro, TN

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.
