    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Murfreesboro

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds.     Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Murfreesboro, TN

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BGV Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.
