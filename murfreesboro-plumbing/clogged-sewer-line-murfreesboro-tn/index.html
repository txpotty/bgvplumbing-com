    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Murfreesboro, TN

    Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can crack buried pipes over time. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Water and solids sit in the dip. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. Then we quote a spot repair.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Murfreesboro, TN

    One slow drain is a branch clog. More than one usually means the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, which is why we ask which fixtures are acting up before we decide where to open the line. The right entry point saves time.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Murfreesboro

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale while the nozzle drives itself forward. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Murfreesboro

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.
