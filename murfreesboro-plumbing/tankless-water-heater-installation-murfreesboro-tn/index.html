    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Murfreesboro

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Murfreesboro, TN

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.
