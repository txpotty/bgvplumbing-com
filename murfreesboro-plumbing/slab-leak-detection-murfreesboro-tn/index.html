    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Murfreesboro, TN

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Murfreesboro, TN home. We have specific methods for each.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Murfreesboro

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Murfreesboro

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Murfreesboro

    A sudden drop in water pressure, visible dampness or the sound of running water can signal an emergency slab leak. BGV Plumbing offers emergency detection services in Murfreesboro to address urgent situations quickly. We prioritize rapid response to limit water damage and prevent further structural compromise to your property.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Murfreesboro

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Murfreesboro

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Murfreesboro homes.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Murfreesboro, TN

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Murfreesboro home.
