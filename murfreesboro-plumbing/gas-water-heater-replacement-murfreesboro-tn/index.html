    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Murfreesboro, TN Home

    No Hot Water Calls in Murfreesboro

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Murfreesboro, TN

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. We keep your downtime short.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Murfreesboro

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Murfreesboro

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Murfreesboro, TN

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.
