    What We Replace in Murfreesboro, TN Houses

    Water Heater Replacement in Murfreesboro

    Water heaters in Murfreesboro that leak from the tank, rumble on every heat cycle or run out of hot water halfway through a shower are usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing. A leak from the tank body itself cannot be fixed. New units get matched for fuel and size, with venting updates plus an expansion tank or pan if code requires it. Hot water is back by evening.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Murfreesboro

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. Replacing the tank stops it. We also check the switch.

    Water Line Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    A leaking water service line wastes water around the clock and can soften the soil next to the foundation. In Murfreesboro, TN, we replace old galvanized, polybutylene and failing copper service lines with PEX or copper from the meter to the house and add a new full-port main shutoff at the foundation wall. One day covers most.

    Water Softener Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Softeners wear out as the resin breaks down and the control valve stops cycling properly. Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have fairly hard water. Once scale returns, dishes spot or salt stops dropping, we test hardness, size a new unit and plumb it with a bypass so the house keeps water during service. New valves use less salt.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    A dead sump pump during a storm floods a basement fast, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Murfreesboro, TN, we replace sump pumps with cast iron or thermoplastic models sized for your inflow, add a check valve, and recommend a battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and the pressure switch setting. We run each one through cycles.

    Gas Line Replacement in Murfreesboro

    Old black iron or failing gas pipe in Murfreesboro homes can leak at threaded joints, and a gas leak is not something to wait on. Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas company from outside, and once it is safe we replace the bad section or the full run, test it and relight everything. Testing comes before relight.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Murfreesboro

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Murfreesboro leads to rusty water and expensive leaks. Failing sections or the whole house get new PEX or copper, routed to keep openings small and pressure tested long enough to catch any slow drip. Patching is included.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Murfreesboro

    Sewer lines in Murfreesboro fail from roots, cracks, collapses and bellies where the pipe sank and now holds waste. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. We run a camera first. That footage shows exactly where the line failed and how much of it is bad, so you know whether a spot repair, a trenchless liner or a full replacement makes the most sense for your yard.
