    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Murfreesboro, TN

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media.     Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Tennessee. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components.     Brine Tank Replacement in Murfreesboro

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore steady brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.
