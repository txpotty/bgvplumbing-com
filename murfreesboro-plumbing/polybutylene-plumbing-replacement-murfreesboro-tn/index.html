    How We Replace Polybutylene in Murfreesboro, TN

    PEX Pipe Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Murfreesboro

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. We replace those lines with PEX sized for the home and secure them properly to the frame. Exposed lines get checked. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually unhook garden hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX is the cheaper option.     Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Murfreesboro, TN

    Some homeowners in Murfreesboro, TN want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Murfreesboro

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.
