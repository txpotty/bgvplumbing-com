    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro

    Direct vent water heaters draw combustion air from outside and vent exhaust through a sealed system. They are a good choice for tight utility rooms or interior closets. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall with proper clearances. Many direct vent models do not need electricity. They offer safe, dependable operation.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Power vent water heaters use a fan to push exhaust through PVC or other approved vent pipe. This allows installation in places without a chimney or vertical flue. Power vent units need an electrical outlet nearby. We install the vent run to manufacturer specs and check for proper termination. These units offer flexible placement options.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro

    Atmospheric vent water heaters rely on natural draft to move exhaust through a metal flue. They are common in older homes and are often the most affordable gas option. We inspect the existing vent for damage, proper slope and clearance. If the vent is undersized or corroded, we replace it. Proper draft is essential for safety.
