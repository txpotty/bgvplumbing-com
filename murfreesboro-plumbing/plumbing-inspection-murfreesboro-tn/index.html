    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Murfreesboro, TN

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Murfreesboro, TN

    Older homes in Murfreesboro often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. We mark it on the ground. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Murfreesboro

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Water Heater Inspections in Murfreesboro

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Murfreesboro

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. That leaves room to negotiate. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Murfreesboro, TN

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Bigger properties cost more. Next to a surprise sewer replacement at $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection costs little, and the price is set when you schedule.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Murfreesboro

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Tennessee houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. Each gets tested, not guessed at. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Murfreesboro

    Businesses in Murfreesboro use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. We look at backflow assemblies, water heaters, restroom flush valves, floor drains and trap primers and the building sewer. Downtime costs money. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Murfreesboro

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.
