    Water Heater Leak Repair in Murfreesboro, TN

    Water on the floor next to the heater needs a closer look before anyone panics. Leaks at the relief valve or a supply fitting are usually cheap to fix. Tank body leaks are different, because a failed inner lining cannot be patched. On a slab floor, a drain pan buys time.

    Electric Tank Repair in Murfreesboro, TN

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Hard water piles scale on the lower element until it burns out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Murfreesboro

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. Hard water can eat through one in a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. Most no-heat calls trace to the thermocouple or the gas control. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.
