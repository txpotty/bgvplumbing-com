    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Murfreesboro, TN

    Sprinkler heads sit in fertilizer and standing water, and a pressure drop on the main can siphon that water back into the house. A pressure vacuum breaker stops back-siphonage on most residential systems, and we mount it at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head so it can do its job in Murfreesboro, TN. Height is the code detail people miss.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Murfreesboro, TN. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. We check clearance and drainage too.

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks.
