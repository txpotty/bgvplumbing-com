    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Murfreesboro

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Murfreesboro

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way.     Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath.     Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Murfreesboro, TN

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first.
