    Tank and Tankless Water Heaters in Murfreesboro, TN

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Murfreesboro

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. We follow them exactly. Your warranty stays intact.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Murfreesboro

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Murfreesboro

    Most installs in Murfreesboro, TN land between $1,200 and $3,500. Tank versus tankless, size, gas versus electric and any needed upgrades all move the number. Hard access costs more. The price is set before we begin.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    A tank heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years with maintenance. Tankless units often go 15 to 20 years because they do not sit full of hot water and their parts can be replaced.

    Permits are common for water heater work. Tennessee adopts the International Plumbing Code at the state level, and many local jurisdictions enforce their own adopted edition with local amendments. BGV Plumbing handles the permit and the inspection in Murfreesboro.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. You will know before we start.
