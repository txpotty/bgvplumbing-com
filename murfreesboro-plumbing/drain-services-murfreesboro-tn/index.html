    Sewer Line Cleaning and Root Removal in Murfreesboro

    Tree roots seek moisture and slip into clay and cast iron sewer lines through joints and hairline cracks. Over time they form dense mats that catch everything flushed down the line. We cut roots with a sectional machine and blade, then jet the line to flush out debris. A foaming root treatment can slow regrowth for a year or more. If roots have cracked or crushed the pipe, we show you the camera footage and talk through repair.

    Hydro Jetting in Murfreesboro

    Hydro jetting sends water at up to 4,000 psi through a specialized nozzle that scours the full inside wall of the pipe. Unlike a cable, which punches a hole through the clog, jetting removes grease, scale and fine roots completely. It is the go-to method for restaurant drains and lines that clog again within months. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough for jetting. Most residential jetting jobs take two to three hours.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Murfreesboro

    The cost of drain cleaning in Murfreesboro can vary a great deal based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after inspecting the issue.

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an estimated timeline after our initial assessment.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear blockages and scour the inside of pipes. It's often needed for severe clogs, tree root intrusions, grease buildup, or when traditional snaking isn't effective, particularly for main sewer lines.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, effectively removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to withstand the process.

    Most single fixture drain cleanings take under an hour. Main line cleaning and hydro jetting typically take one to three hours.

    Minor drain cleaning usually does not require a permit. However, significant work like sewer line repair or replacement, especially if it involves excavation or connecting to the city's main sewer, typically requires permits from the local building department. We will advise you and take care of the necessary permitting.

    When it comes to drain services, our focus is on clearing and repairing the pipes themselves, rather than specific fixture brands. However, our technicians are familiar with all major plumbing fixture brands like Moen, Delta, Kohler, and American Standard, and can address any issues related to their drain assemblies during service.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer and more effective.
