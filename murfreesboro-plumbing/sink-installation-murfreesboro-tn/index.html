    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.
