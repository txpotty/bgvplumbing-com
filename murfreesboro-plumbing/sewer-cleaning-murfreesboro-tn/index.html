    Main Line and Sewer Clearing in Murfreesboro, TN

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Murfreesboro

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Murfreesboro, TN

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Murfreesboro

    Busy buildings clog more often. More use means more buildup. We bring larger jetters for 4 and 6 inch commercial lines and can work before opening or after closing, so the cleaning does not shut down a lunch rush or a full day of business.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Murfreesboro, TN

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Murfreesboro, TN

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Murfreesboro

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Root Masses in Murfreesboro Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. We cut roots in several passes, then look on camera to judge the damage and talk about lining or a spot repair.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Murfreesboro, TN

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.
