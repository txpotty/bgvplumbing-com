    New Sump Pit Excavation in Murfreesboro

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Sump Pump Pit Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    A well-built sump pit is fundamental to an effective sump pump system. If your basement lacks a suitable pit or needs an upgrade, BGV Plumbing provides professional sump pit installation. We carefully excavate and prepare the pit to the correct dimensions and depth for proper drainage and pump function. This foundational step is critical for any new sump pump installation and prevents the sediment buildup that can damage the pump.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Murfreesboro

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Wayne Sump Pump Installation in Murfreesboro, TN

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Murfreesboro

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water effectively. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not re-enter your Murfreesboro property.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Murfreesboro

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Murfreesboro

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. BGV Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Murfreesboro home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.
