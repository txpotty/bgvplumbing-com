    Annual Backflow Testing in Murfreesboro

    Most water providers require backflow assemblies to be tested at least once a year. Our certified testers connect a calibrated differential pressure gauge and check each check valve and relief valve. Test results are recorded on the provider's form and submitted for you. If the assembly passes, you are done until next year.

    Backflow Preventer Repair and Rebuild in Murfreesboro, TN

    Common failures include worn check valve rubbers, debris stuck under seats and relief valves that drip constantly. We isolate the assembly, disassemble it and replace the worn parts with the manufacturer rebuild kit. Then we retest to verify it passes. Rebuilding usually costs far less than replacing the entire assembly.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Murfreesboro

    Sprinkler and drip systems are one of the most common sources of cross-connection risk because they sit in soil with fertilizers and pesticides. Many systems use a pressure vacuum breaker or a reduced pressure assembly installed above grade. We size and install the correct device and set it at the proper height.     Freeze and Heat Protection for Backflow Preventers in Murfreesboro, TN

    Above-ground backflow assemblies are exposed to summer sun and winter cold. Protecting the assembly helps it pass testing and last longer.
