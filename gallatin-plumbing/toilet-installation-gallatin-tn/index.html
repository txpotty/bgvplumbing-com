    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Gallatin

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits.     Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Gallatin

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Gallatin

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Gallatin

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring.
