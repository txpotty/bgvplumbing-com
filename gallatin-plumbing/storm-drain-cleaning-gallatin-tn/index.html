    Common signs of a clogged storm drain include standing water in your yard or driveway after rain, water overflowing from catch basins, odd pooling near downspouts, or slow drainage in outdoor areas.     In Gallatin, common causes of storm drain clogs include built-up leaves, dirt, and organic debris from trees, silt and sediment from soil erosion, trash, and unfortunately, sometimes even illegally dumped waste.     In Gallatin, storm drains are commonly clogged by natural debris such as leaves, twigs, dirt, and silt, especially after heavy winds or storms.
