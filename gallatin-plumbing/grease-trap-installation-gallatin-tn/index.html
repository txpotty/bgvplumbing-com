    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Gallatin

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Gallatin, TN

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Tennessee ordinances for waste management.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Gallatin

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties. Brands like Thermaco Big Dipper are popular in kitchens with limited space and frequent cleaning needs. We install these units with the correct power supply and drain connections. They can reduce the need for frequent pump-outs in smaller kitchens. We explain daily maintenance so your staff keeps the unit running well.
