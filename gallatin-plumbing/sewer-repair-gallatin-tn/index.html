    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Gallatin, TN

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more common in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Gallatin, TN

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to serious damage and unsanitary conditions.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Gallatin

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Gallatin

    The cost of sewer repair in Gallatin, TN can vary a lot, typically ranging from $300 for a simple clog removal to $6,000 or more for extensive line replacement using trenchless methods. Factors include the extent of damage, pipe material, accessibility and whether excavation is needed. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This protects your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.

    Key signs you need sewer repair include multiple slow drains or frequent backups, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul sewer odors in or around your home, soggy spots in your yard or an unexplained increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call for an inspection.

    The length of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Trenchless repairs like pipe lining can often be completed in one to two days. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    For sewer line replacements, we primarily use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your needs and local soil conditions.

    Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smells in the yard and wet or unusually green patches over the line. Water backing up into a tub or shower when you run the washing machine is another classic warning sign.

    We use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS that meets local code, joined with approved fittings and shielded no-hub couplings at transitions. For lining, we use epoxy-saturated liners rated for many years of service.

    We work with all common types of sewer pipe found in Gallatin and Tennessee, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, clay and Orangeburg. Our technicians are skilled in repairing and replacing lines made from various materials, providing compatibility and durability with current plumbing standards. We also use high-quality, code-compliant replacement materials.
