    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Gallatin, TN

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Gallatin, TN

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. The pump runs on a timer. Mornings and evenings, usually.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Gallatin

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Gallatin

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. Starved units throw error codes.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Gallatin

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in Gallatin, TN

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (615) 518-0893 any time it quits. We stock common models for same-day replacement. If your exact unit is back-ordered, a temporary repair can sometimes hold until it ships.

    Navien Venting and Install in Gallatin

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Warranty depends on it.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Gallatin, TN

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.
