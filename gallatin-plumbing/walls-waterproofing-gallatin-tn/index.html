    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Gallatin

    Many homes in Gallatin have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Gallatin

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Gallatin

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Gallatin, TN

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Gallatin

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more frequent in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Gallatin, TN

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during heavy storms. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Gallatin

    Homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which have a history of failure and are a common repipe target. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks.     A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take several days.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is highly advisable.

    Skilled DIYers can roll on liquid membranes, but mistakes at seams, corners and drains often lead to leaks. Shower pans also require correct slope and flood testing. Professional installation lowers the risk of hidden water damage.
