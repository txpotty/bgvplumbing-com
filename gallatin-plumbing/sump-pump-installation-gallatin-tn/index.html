    Sump Pump Installs in Gallatin, TN

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Gallatin

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Gallatin, TN

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Gallatin, TN

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Gallatin

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Gallatin, TN

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Gallatin

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Gallatin, TN

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Gallatin, TN

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.
