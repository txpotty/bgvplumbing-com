    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Gallatin

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    We install Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and other reliable brands. We recommend models based on your pit size and water inflow.

    A simple replacement often does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, well suited for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    To keep your new sump pump running well, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates. Once a year, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from BGV Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Gallatin home.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so periodic testing is important.
