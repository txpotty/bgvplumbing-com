    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Smyrna, TN

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Smyrna, TN.     In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Smyrna, TN

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can break buried pipes over time. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Smyrna

    The cost of grease trap installation in Smyrna, TN varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap. A small under-sink unit might take a few hours, while a large outdoor interceptor could require several days due to excavation and concrete work. We strive to complete installations efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    The cleaning frequency for a new grease trap depends on its size, the volume of FOG produced by your kitchen, and local regulations in Smyrna. Typically, grease traps require cleaning every 1 to 3 months. Regular cleaning is essential to maintain efficiency and prevent backups.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are usually rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you choose the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.
