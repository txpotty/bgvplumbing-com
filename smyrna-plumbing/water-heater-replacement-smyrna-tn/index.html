    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a sensible option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best handled by licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. BGV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.
