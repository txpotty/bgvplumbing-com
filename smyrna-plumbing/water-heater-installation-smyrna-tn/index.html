    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Smyrna

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Tennessee adopts the International Plumbing Code at the state level, and many local jurisdictions enforce their own adopted edition with local amendments. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Smyrna.
