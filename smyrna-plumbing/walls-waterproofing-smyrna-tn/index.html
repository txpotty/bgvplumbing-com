    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Smyrna, TN

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Smyrna

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Smyrna

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Rapid swings between mild and freezing weather in Smyrna can stress joints and fittings over time as pipes expand and contract. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Smyrna, TN

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst.     Interior Walls Waterproofing in Smyrna, TN

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Smyrna

    Homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which have a history of failure and are a common repipe target. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Smyrna

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. We backfill after the drain is tested.
