    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Smyrna, TN

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Smyrna, TN

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Smyrna, TN

    Leaks are a frequent issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. We examine the drain pan and surrounding area for moisture. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more destructive problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Smyrna

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.
