    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Smyrna, TN

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Smyrna, TN

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Smyrna

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
