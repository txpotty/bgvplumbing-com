    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Smyrna

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Smyrna

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. We dig where the noise is loudest.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Smyrna, TN

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Smyrna

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Smyrna, TN

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Smyrna

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Smyrna

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Fast growth around Smyrna means many newer subdivisions, but older neighborhoods still rely on aging clay or cast iron sewer laterals. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.
