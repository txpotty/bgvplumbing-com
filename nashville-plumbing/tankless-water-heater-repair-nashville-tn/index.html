    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Nashville, TN

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Nashville, TN are adept at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and fixing issues with gas valve assemblies. We ensure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Nashville

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Nashville

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.
