    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable.     Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Nashville

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Nashville for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Nashville

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Nashville, TN

BGV Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Nashville, TN so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is important for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.
