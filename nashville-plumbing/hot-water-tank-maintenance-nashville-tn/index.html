    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Nashville, TN

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Nashville

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Nashville, TN

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Nashville, TN, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.
