    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Nashville

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be very annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to allow a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Nashville

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, comfortable temperature.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Nashville

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in Nashville, TN

    Many homes in Nashville, TN feature tub and shower combination units, which means their plumbing is connected. Issues like faulty diverter valves, shared drain clogs, or a leaky tub spout can affect the entire unit. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing and repairing problems specific to these combination fixtures, ensuring both your tub and shower function perfectly. We handle all components, from the faucet to the overflow drain.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Nashville, TN

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Nashville, TN

    Many homes in Nashville feature Moen or Delta shower fixtures, known for their quality but still susceptible to wear and tear. Our plumbers are highly experienced in repairing and sourcing parts for these popular brands. Whether it is a leaky single-handle cartridge or a diverter issue, we have the specific knowledge to fix your Moen or Delta shower correctly.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Nashville

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers promptly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Nashville

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are skilled in diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.
