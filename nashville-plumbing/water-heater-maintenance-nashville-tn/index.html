    Anode Rod Replacement in Nashville

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Nashville, TN

    Small drips become floods.     The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
