    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Nashville

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. The alarm sounds because liquid climbed past the high float. We check the breaker and meter the pump draw before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Nashville

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and what we do next depends on what comes out of the pipe. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Nashville

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Nashville

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. That section sags or breaks from roots and settling soil. A camera from the house cleanout finds the bad joint, and we mark it in the yard. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Nashville, TN

    Some tanks have no riser, so we look at the baffles through the small inspection port with a push camera. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Repair Versus Replace in Nashville, TN

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. You see the repair and the replacement priced next to each other, and we tell you which one we would pick for our own house. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.
