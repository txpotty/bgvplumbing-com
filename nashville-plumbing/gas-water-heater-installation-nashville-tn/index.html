    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashville

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashville, TN

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. BGV Plumbing installs the full Rheem line. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Draft is checked last.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashville

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashville

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. Strapping where required. Sizing follows household demand.
