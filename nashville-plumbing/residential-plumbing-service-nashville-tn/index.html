    Residential Plumbing Service in Nashville, TN: The Work We Do

    Water Heater Repair and Installs in Nashville

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest. We work on gas, electric, tank and tankless heaters from Rheem and Bradford White, and the usual culprits are a heavy layer of sediment or a thermocouple that has quit reading the pilot. No hot water is often a quick part swap. Replacements go in with an expansion tank and drain pan where code calls for them.

    Gas Line Repair and Installation in Nashville, TN

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us. We run new black iron or CSST lines for ranges, dryers and grills, then pressure-test the line and check every joint with a leak detector before the appliance gets lit. All gas work is permitted where required.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Clogs in Nashville

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. A single slow sink is usually a trap or branch clog, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Nashville, TN

    Water stains on a ceiling rarely sit right under the leak. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can break buried pipes over time. We trace leaks with acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and isolation pressure tests, which lets us open one small hole in the right spot instead of cutting drywall all over the house. Repairs follow the same day when possible.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Nashville, TN

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Nashville

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Most slow drains come down to grease, hair or wipes, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. We send you the video file.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. We service Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai units, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. We haul the old tank away.
