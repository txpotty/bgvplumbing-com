    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Replacement in Nashville

    Sewer line replacement costs in Nashville, TN can range significantly, typically from $4,000 to $25,000 or more, depending on factors like the length of the line, depth, pipe material, chosen method (traditional vs. trenchless), and excavation complexity.     Trenchless replacements often take one day. Open-trench jobs can take two to four days.

    Frequent backups, slow drains throughout the house, sewage odors and soggy yard areas are typical signs. Camera inspection verifies the problem.

    Frequent sewage backups, multiple clogged drains, foul odors coming from drains or your yard, lush green patches in your lawn, indentations in your yard, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use are all clear indicators of a failing sewer line. Persistent problems often point to the need for replacement rather than just repairs.

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. Shifting soil can crack pipes, and tree roots, attracted to the moisture and nutrients, will infiltrate these cracks, growing inside the pipe and causing severe blockages and even collapses.

    For sewer replacement, we mainly use durable and long-lasting materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) and ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes. These materials are resistant to corrosion, tree root intrusion, and chemical damage, offering a much longer lifespan compared to older clay or cast iron pipes commonly found in Nashville homes. We select the best material for your specific property and local conditions.

    BGV Plumbing offers comprehensive warranties on both the materials we install and our workmanship for sewer replacement services.
