    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail without warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This lowers the chance of sudden leaks.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Nashville

    Sometimes, traditional open-trench excavation is the most appropriate method for water line replacement in Nashville, especially for severe damage, complex pipe layouts, or when the existing pipe material is incompatible with trenchless techniques. Our team carefully excavates the area, removes the old pipe, and installs a new, high-quality main water line, typically using copper or PEX. We ensure proper grading and compaction of the soil during backfilling to avoid future issues.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Nashville

    Copper has long been a preferred material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. BGV Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and limit bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a dependable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Nashville, TN.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Nashville, TN

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it very durable for most water conditions. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually unhook garden hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a real advantage when a cold snap hits Nashville. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.

    Beyond simple replacement, we also offer water line upgrades to improve water flow or ensure compliance with current plumbing codes.     Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Nashville

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.
