    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Nashville

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Nashville

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Plumbing work above certain thresholds requires a state licensed contractor, and BGV Plumbing handles permits so homeowners do not have to figure it out alone. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Nashville, TN

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Nashville

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Nashville

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Nashville, TN

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Nashville, TN

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
