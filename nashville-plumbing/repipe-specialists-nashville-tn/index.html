    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Nashville, TN

    Copper Repiping in Nashville, TN

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Nashville, TN

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    Commercial Repiping in Nashville, TN

    Commercial buildings in Nashville, TN often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Nashville, TN

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. We match the texture. Knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes are all routine, and your walls are ready for primer and paint when we leave.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Nashville

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Nashville

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Nashville

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.
