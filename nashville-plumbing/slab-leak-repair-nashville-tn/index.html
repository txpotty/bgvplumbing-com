    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Nashville, TN

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX flexes with the house, does not corrode and can be run through the attic and walls, so a full repipe replaces every supply line and takes the slab out of the picture. It suits many Nashville homes.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Nashville

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold lines show no heat. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Nashville truck.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Nashville

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Nashville home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Nashville

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Nashville, TN

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. Find your main valve. We help you close the main by phone, and a tech then isolates the leaking line so the rest of the house gets water back.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Nashville, TN

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Nashville, TN

    Commercial slabs in Nashville carry longer pipe runs and more branches. Locating takes longer. We isolate branches one at a time, locate the leak and schedule cutting for after hours or a weekend, so your doors stay open and your customers never see a saw. Work meets Tennessee code and stays within code.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Nashville, TN

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.
