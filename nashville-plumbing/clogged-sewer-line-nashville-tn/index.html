    Hydro-Jetting Services in Nashville, TN

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an excellent preventative maintenance tool and is particularly effective for older pipes in Nashville that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Nashville

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Nashville

    Some older homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces plumbers to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent to clear the main line. We install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so future clearing is faster and cheaper. The cleanout also gives the city or a future plumber easy access for inspections. This is a common upgrade after a serious sewer clog.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Service in Nashville, TN

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain needs immediate attention. Our emergency crews stop the backup, clear the line and help contain the mess. We can refer you to a cleanup and restoration crew if sewage reached carpet or drywall. We also provide photos and notes for insurance claims.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Nashville

    Many properties in Nashville feature mature trees, whose roots are a leading cause of sewer line clogs. Tree roots naturally seek water and can work into even tiny cracks in sewer pipes, growing into dense masses that block flow. We use specialized cutting tools and high-pressure hydro-jetting to fully remove these root intrusions. Following removal, we can inspect the pipe with a camera to assess damage and recommend long-term solutions, like pipe lining or replacement.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Nashville

    Routine clearing does not require a permit. Cleanout installation, spot repairs and full replacement typically do. We handle permits and inspections when they apply.

    Cutting roots clears the line but roots will grow back if the joints remain open. Root treatments can slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement seals the joints for good. We can show you the extent of root intrusion on camera.

    Most main line clearings take one to two hours. Severe root intrusion or lines without a cleanout can take longer. Camera inspection adds about 30 to 60 minutes.

    Attempting to clear a main sewer line clog yourself is not recommended. Over-the-counter drain cleaners can damage your pipes, and without the right tools like sewer cameras or hydro-jetters, you risk pushing the clog further or causing more damage. Professional plumbers have the specialized equipment and expertise to safely and effectively clear these blockages.

    Rental drum augers can be dangerous and may get stuck or damage old pipe. Main sewer clogs also involve sewage exposure. It is safer and often more effective to call a professional.

    In Nashville, TN, common causes include tree root intrusion, buildup of grease and foreign objects (wipes, feminine hygiene products), shifting soil causing pipe misalignment, and aging pipes that corrode or collapse.
