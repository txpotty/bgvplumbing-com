    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Nashville, TN

    Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can crack buried pipes over time. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, and the line clogs at the same spot every few months no matter how often it gets cleaned. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. Then we quote a spot repair.

    Commercial Sewer Line Clearing in Nashville, TN

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Nashville, TN

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Nashville, TN

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Nashville

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. A hairline gap is enough. Once inside, they spread into a net that traps everything moving through. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, then camera the line to see how many joints are letting roots in. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Nashville, TN

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Then it grows. Soap scum and food bind to it until the opening shrinks to a fraction of the pipe, and at that point even normal use backs the line up into the lowest fixture in the house. BGV Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in Nashville, TN. The camera confirms it is gone.
