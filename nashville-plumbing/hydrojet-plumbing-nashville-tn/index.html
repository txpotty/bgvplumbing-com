    Drain and Sewer Jetting Services in Nashville, TN

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Nashville, TN

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Nashville, TN

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Flat runs collect more debris. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Nashville

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. On-call trucks are fully equipped. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Nashville, TN

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Nashville

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. We set up outside at the cleanout whenever we can, so the jetter hose never has to come through your house or across your floors. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Nashville

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    Root Removal Jetting in Nashville

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Nashville, TN

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.
