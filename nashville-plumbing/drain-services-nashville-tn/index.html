    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Nashville

    The cost of drain cleaning in Nashville can vary a great deal based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the issue.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, effectively removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to withstand the process.

    A camera inspection is worth it if clogs keep coming back, you are buying an older home or roots are suspected. It shows the exact location and cause of the problem.

    While readily available, we generally advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer for your pipes and more effective.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains, foul odors coming from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and multiple fixtures draining slowly. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call a professional before the problem escalates to a full blockage or a sewage backup.

    Minor drain cleaning usually does not require a permit. However, significant work like sewer line repair or replacement, especially if it involves excavation or connecting to the city's main sewer, typically requires permits from the local building department. We will advise you and take care of the necessary permitting.

    When it comes to drain services, our focus is on clearing and repairing the pipes themselves, rather than specific fixture brands. However, our technicians are familiar with all major plumbing fixture brands like Moen, Delta, Kohler, and American Standard, and can address any issues related to their drain assemblies during service.

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an expected timeline after our initial assessment.
