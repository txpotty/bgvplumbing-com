    Pipe Bursting Services in Nashville, TN

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Nashville, TN soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Nashville

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Nashville yard.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Nashville, TN

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Then we schedule the fix. If a liner is a good fit, we book the repair quickly, keep the line flowing where possible in the meantime and tell you what to avoid flushing until the work is complete.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Nashville

    CIPP lining typically costs $80 to $250 per foot. Bursting runs a bit less per foot. Pipe bursting often runs $60 to $200 per foot plus the access pits, and a typical residential sewer line totals $4,000 to $15,000 depending on length and condition.

    Lining and bursting are rated for 50 years or more. The materials last. Both resist rust, roots and chemicals much better than old clay or cast iron, which explains the long rating.

    Parts and labor are both covered. Length depends on the method. The specific warranty period depends on whether we lined, spot-lined or burst the pipe and which materials went in, and you get the full details in writing during your consultation.

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Bursting replaces it. With CIPP, a resin-soaked liner cures against the old pipe wall, while bursting breaks the old pipe apart and pulls new HDPE into its place, leaving a completely new pipe.

    Slow drains all over the house, not just one fixture. Smells are another. Lush strips of lawn, damp spots in the yard and a water bill that jumps can also point to a broken line, and in older homes, a camera inspection is a good idea before the problem turns into a backup.

    CIPP lining jobs usually take one to three days. Bursting can run longer. Bursting can take a bit more depending on length, and both beat open trenching, which can run weeks with restoration.

    An epoxy liner, correctly installed, is built to last 50 years or more. HDPE pipe used in bursting has a similar long service life.

    Perma-Liner for lining and Picote for pipe prep. Both are well proven. We picked them because the liners cure into a structural pipe and the Picote cutters clean and reopen lateral connections accurately, which is where a lot of lining jobs go wrong.
