    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Nashville

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Nashville, TN

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Nashville

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Nashville, TN

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Nashville, TN

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Nashville, TN

    A standard tank replacement costs the least up front in Nashville, TN. Tankless and hybrid heat pump models cost more to buy and install but can cut operating costs over the life of the unit, and tankless units often last 15 to 20 years compared to roughly 8 to 12 for a typical tank. We price all three.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Nashville

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. You shower tonight.
