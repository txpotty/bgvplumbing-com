    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Nashville

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawlspaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens. These runs freeze first. After repairing a split, we look at whether the line can be moved into a heated space, buried under attic insulation on the warm side, or wrapped with heat cable, since the same pipe will freeze again otherwise.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Nashville

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.

    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in Nashville

    Pipes under a slab rarely freeze, but the lines where they rise out of the slab at an exterior wall can. The riser is where trouble starts. Acoustic and thermal tools find slab-area leaks without breaking up the floor first.

    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Nashville

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line. We open the faucet and find the frozen section. Metal pipe thaws fast with electric current, and plastic lines get slower, gentler heat. Patience pays here.

    Freeze Protection for Commercial Buildings in Nashville, TN

    Vacant suites and warehouses freeze when the heat gets turned down too far. We set up freeze plans. That plan lists the shutoffs, the at-risk runs, the heat cable and the minimum thermostat setting for each space, so building staff know exactly what to do when a cold front is in the forecast.

    Emergency Frozen Pipe Service in Nashville

    If water is spraying or pouring from a ceiling, shut off the main valve and call us. Turn off the water heater too. If the house lines drain down, a water heater left firing can be damaged, so we walk you through that over the phone and get a plumber to you as fast as conditions allow.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Nashville, TN

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a hose bib with the hose left on. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow preventers sit above ground with no insulation, and their brass bodies and test cocks crack easily, so we drain or cover them before the first freeze and repair them when they split.

    Pool, Outdoor Equipment and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in Nashville, TN

    Pool equipment lines, outdoor kitchen sinks and ice maker tubing in garages are easy to forget until they crack. Pool pumps often run to prevent freezing. A power outage on a cold night puts pool and outdoor lines at risk, and we repair the cracked fittings.
