    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Nashville

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Nashville residence.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Nashville, TN

    Many Nashville, TN homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. BGV Plumbing provides essential maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a real concern during severe storms.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Nashville

    BGV Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, confirming the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for efficient repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Nashville, TN

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, BGV Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Nashville, TN. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Nashville

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Nashville

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Nashville

    BGV Plumbing is skilled in maintaining all major sump pump brands, including popular models from Zoeller and Wayne. Our technicians understand the specific components and maintenance requirements for these trusted brands, so your unit receives expert care. Whether it's a submersible Zoeller pump or a pedestal Wayne pump, we provide thorough service to keep it running smoothly for your Nashville home.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Nashville

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to confirm it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Nashville home from unexpected flooding.
