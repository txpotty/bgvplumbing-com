    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Nashville, TN

    Salt-based water softeners are the most common and effective type, using ion exchange to remove hard minerals like calcium and magnesium. BGV Plumbing specializes in the installation of these traditional systems, making sure of proper brine tank setup and regeneration cycles.     Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Nashville

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet.
