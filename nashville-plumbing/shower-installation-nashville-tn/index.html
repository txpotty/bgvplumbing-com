    Shower Stall Installation in Nashville

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Nashville

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Nashville

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. Then the valve. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Nashville, TN

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. Spend on the valve. The quote covers everything up front.

    Digital Shower Systems in Nashville, TN

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. An electrician handles the outlet. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Nashville, TN

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Nashville, TN

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.
