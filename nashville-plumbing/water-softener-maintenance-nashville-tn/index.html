    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Nashville

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts happen more often on commercial units.

    Commercial Softener Service in Nashville

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set a service schedule around your hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Nashville

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Nashville, TN

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Softener Maintenance Plans and Pricing in Nashville, TN

    The plan is one flat yearly fee. It covers the service visit and both hardness tests, and parts installed on a plan visit come at a reduced price. Catching one stuck float or clogged injector early usually covers the fee.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Nashville, TN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Nashville, TN

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Nashville, TN

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.
