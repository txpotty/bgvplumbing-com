    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Nashville, TN

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Nashville, TN need lateral work too.     How Pipe Bursting Works in Nashville

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. Only two pits get dug. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in Nashville

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. We get a camera in, find the failure, mark it, and tell you the same day whether we can dig the next morning or need to wait for utility locates to clear. Utility marking takes a few days in most areas. A temporary bypass can keep a bathroom working in the meantime.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Nashville, TN

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem.     Residential Sewer Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more frequent in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes.     Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Nashville, TN

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. A typical burst takes one working day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.
