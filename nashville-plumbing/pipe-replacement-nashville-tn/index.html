    What Goes Into a Repipe in Nashville, TN

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, so we fix it and protect it from the next freeze. Then we test it.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Some water is harder on pipe. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Nashville, TN

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, so we avoid tearing open walls just to pull out dead pipe. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Nashville, TN

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    PEX Repiping in Nashville

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Nashville

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Nashville

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, and usually costs less than repairing in place. Fast too.
