    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Nashville, TN

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones.     Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Nashville

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We space urinals 30 inches apart and 15 inches from side walls, then set carriers and run all the lines. Coordination with the GC is constant. Rough inspection comes first.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Nashville, TN

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go.     Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Nashville

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on.     Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Nashville, TN

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush.     Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Nashville, TN

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Nashville, TN

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. We install them with the correct inside parts kit for the bowl's rated volume, set the control stop so the flush is strong without splashing, and keep rebuild kits on the truck for later service. Sensor versions mount the same way. Battery models need no wiring.
