    Lasting Grease Trap Repair Services in Nashville, TN

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Nashville, TN

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Nashville, TN

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are essential for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Nashville, TN, ensuring your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Emergency Grease Trap Repair in Nashville

    A failed trap during a busy shift can force you to stop service. We respond quickly to grease trap emergencies and work to get you running the same day when possible. Our crews carry common replacement parts and pumping equipment. We can pump, repair and test the trap in one visit in many cases. Quick service limits lost revenue and health code concerns.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Nashville

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Nashville

    Persistent foul odors coming from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth. Beyond cleaning, our repair services address the root cause, whether it's poor ventilation, sludge buildup, or structural issues preventing proper flow. We make sure all components are working correctly to contain and process waste effectively, eliminating unpleasant smells that can deter customers in Nashville.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Nashville

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. If the body is badly damaged, we recommend replacement.

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections.
