    Shower Diverter Repair in Bartlett, TN

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Bartlett

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model. We offer options from standard to rain showerheads, providing proper installation for strong performance and water conservation.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Bartlett

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Bartlett

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are experienced at diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve makers.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Bartlett

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Bartlett

    A shower that drips after it is turned off typically has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Bartlett, TN

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are usually caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.
