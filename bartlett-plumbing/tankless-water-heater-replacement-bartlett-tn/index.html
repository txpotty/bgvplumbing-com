    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Bartlett

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Plan on four to six hours.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Bartlett, TN

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually disconnect hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. Often the simplest job we do.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Bartlett, TN

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Bartlett, TN

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Navien Venting and Install in Bartlett

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Bartlett

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Most take under two hours.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Bartlett, TN

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Bartlett

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC.
