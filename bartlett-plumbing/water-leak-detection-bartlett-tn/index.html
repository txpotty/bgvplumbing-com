    Slab Leak Detection in Bartlett

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. BGV Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.
