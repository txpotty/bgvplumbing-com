    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. We also check whether you need an expansion tank. Most installs finish in one visit.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Bartlett, TN

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit.     Water Heater Installation in Bartlett

    Tank or tankless, gas or electric, the first question is how many people shower in the morning.     Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bartlett

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Sump Pump Installation in Bartlett, TN

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. The pit goes to proper depth, the primary pump is sized for the inflow, and a battery backup is added on request. Discharge placement matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    PEX Repiping in Bartlett

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. Freeze damage is less likely too. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually disconnect hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Water Softener Installation in Bartlett, TN

    Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We test hardness in grains per gallon at the kitchen tap, then size a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner to your household use. The bypass valve matters. With it, the softener can be serviced while the house still has water.

    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Bartlett

    Corroded galvanized and brittle polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. We run new lines in PEX or Type L copper, using crimp or expansion fittings on the PEX and soldered joints on the copper. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more frequent in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. We bed buried pipe in sand at the right depth so stones and settling soil cannot chafe it over time. Street pressure over 80 psi gets a PRV.
