    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bartlett

    Residential ejector systems in Bartlett serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Bartlett, TN

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Bartlett

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Bartlett

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more frequent in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. Floating basins crack floors.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bartlett, TN

    On a new build, the basin goes in before the slab is poured. With no concrete to cut, it costs the least then, and we stub drains, vent and discharge to the plans for a clean rough-in inspection. Builders like the timing.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Bartlett

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. A full-port valve gets added.
