    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Bartlett

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Troubleshooting in Bartlett

    Even with regular maintenance, now and then unexpected issues arise. BGV Plumbing offers emergency troubleshooting for hot water tank problems in Bartlett.     Commercial and Rental Tank Maintenance in Bartlett

    Property managers, restaurants and small offices often run several tanks that work much harder than a home unit.     Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Bartlett, TN

    Whether your home in Bartlett, TN uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks confirm all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Bartlett

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Bartlett home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Bartlett, TN

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Bartlett

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
