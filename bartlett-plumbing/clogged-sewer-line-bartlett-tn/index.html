    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Bartlett

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Bartlett

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints.     Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does.
