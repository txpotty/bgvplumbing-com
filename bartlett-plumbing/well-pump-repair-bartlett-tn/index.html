    Jet Pump Repair in Bartlett, TN

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Bartlett is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating efficiently again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Bartlett

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Bartlett, TN

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Bartlett, TN

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Bartlett

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.
