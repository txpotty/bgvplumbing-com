    Emergency Sewer Repair in Bartlett

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Bartlett

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Bartlett, TN

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Bartlett

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Tennessee homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bartlett

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Bartlett

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. Night and weekend work is available.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bartlett, TN

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Bartlett, TN

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.
