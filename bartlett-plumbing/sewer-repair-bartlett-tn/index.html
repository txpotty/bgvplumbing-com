    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Bartlett

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Tennessee properties. Our team makes sure of careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Bartlett.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bartlett, TN

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Bartlett.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Bartlett, TN

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. BGV Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Bartlett

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bartlett

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Bartlett dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Bartlett

    Before any repair, we use advanced video camera inspection to accurately locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This no-dig method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a precise diagnosis and repair plan. It's key for understanding issues in older homes in Bartlett and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Bartlett neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe integrity.
