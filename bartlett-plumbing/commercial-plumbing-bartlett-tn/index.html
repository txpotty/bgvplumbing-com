    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Bartlett, TN

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Many 6-inch laterals can be lined in a single day without cutting the parking lot or closing a door.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Bartlett

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. The camera goes in at a cleanout, we note distances to every defect and then locate each one from the surface with a receiver. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Bartlett

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. Trap primers get checked too.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in Bartlett

    Emergencies at a business cost more the longer they last, so speed matters more than the hourly rate. Water on the floor comes first.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Bartlett

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Plumbing Costs and Maintenance Plans in Bartlett, TN

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more.     Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Bartlett

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Bartlett

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can break buried pipes over time. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.
