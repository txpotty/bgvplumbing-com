    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Johnson City, TN

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Johnson City, TN

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Johnson City, TN

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. Often it is just a tripped GFCI or a hung float. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Johnson City, TN

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Johnson City, TN

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Johnson City

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Johnson City

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Johnson City

    Creeks and rivers rise quickly after a heavy rain, so homes in low areas benefit from backwater valves and good yard drainage. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.
