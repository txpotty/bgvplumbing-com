    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Johnson City, TN

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. For outdoor tanks we measure the hose run from where a pump truck can park.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Johnson City

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. The tank holds water for roughly half an hour, which gives grease time to rise and food solids time to drop. We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Johnson City

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Johnson City

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. A trap that sits out of level loses capacity, so we shim it flat before connecting anything.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Johnson City

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. After a few years of hot water and scraping, the lid gasket is what usually leaks. We keep spares on the truck. We mount the supplied flow control on the inlet, at the height the instructions list, and vent it.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Johnson City

    On a new build-out, the grease trap gets decided before the slab is poured, not after. We work from the architect's plumbing sheets and the kitchen equipment schedule. Changes get expensive. Saw-cutting a finished floor to relocate a trap can add several days to a build-out, so we flag sizing or location problems at plan review.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Johnson City, TN

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. Sizing has to cover that short, heavy spike. Ghost kitchens with four or five brands under one roof often share a single trap, so the fixture count can be higher than the floor plan suggests. Each brand's prep sink counts.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Johnson City, TN

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. Plastic tanks in wet soil need hold-down straps or a concrete collar so they do not float when pumped.
