    Split Copper Line Repair in Johnson City, TN

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Water comes fast.     Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Johnson City, TN

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. A regulator often helps.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Johnson City

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Johnson City

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. There is less metal to lose. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. Nearby pipe gets a look.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Johnson City

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. The first sign is often a spot. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.
