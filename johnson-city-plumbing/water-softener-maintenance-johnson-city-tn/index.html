    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Johnson City

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This preventive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Johnson City

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Johnson City.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Johnson City, TN

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Johnson City, TN

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Johnson City homes.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Johnson City, TN

Resin rebedding typically costs $300 to $700 and can bring an older tank back to full capacity. Most quality softeners last 10 to 15 years with service.
