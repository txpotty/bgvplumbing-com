    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Johnson City, TN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold.     Hardness Testing at the Tap in Johnson City

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Johnson City, TN

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Johnson City, TN

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Johnson City, TN

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.
