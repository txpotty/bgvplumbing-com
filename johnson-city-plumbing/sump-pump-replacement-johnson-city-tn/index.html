    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Johnson City

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Johnson City

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. From there it is math. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Johnson City, TN

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Johnson City, TN

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Johnson City

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Johnson City, TN

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Johnson City

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Backup Pump Options in Johnson City, TN

    Heavy spring and fall rains can cause flash flooding in Tennessee, and saturated ground often leads to wet crawlspaces and sewer backups. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. Wells cannot run them.
