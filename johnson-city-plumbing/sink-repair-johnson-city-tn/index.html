    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Johnson City

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Johnson City, TN

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Johnson City

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most vulnerable during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Johnson City

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Johnson City

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Johnson City, TN

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Johnson City

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Finding Sink Repair Near Me in Johnson City, TN
