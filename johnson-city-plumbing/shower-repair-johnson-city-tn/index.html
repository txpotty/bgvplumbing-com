    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints.     Shower Drain Repair in Johnson City

    Slow shower drains are typically clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components when needed.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Johnson City

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Johnson City, TN

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Johnson City

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, comfortable temperature.
