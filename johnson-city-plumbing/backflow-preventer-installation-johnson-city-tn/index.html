    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Johnson City, TN

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most vulnerable during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Johnson City

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Johnson City, TN

    For high-hazard applications in Johnson City, TN where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. BGV Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes.     Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Johnson City

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Johnson City, TN typically ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is usually on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then every year. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    We install backflow preventers from leading manufacturers known for quality and reliability. Our preferred brands include Watts, Febco, Zurn and Apollo, so you receive a durable, effective device that meets all industry standards.

    Most manufacturers offer one to five year warranties depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor and will review the coverage with you.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored or foul-smelling water, a noticeable drop in water pressure, visible leaks around the device or water discharging from the relief valve on an RPZ. If you notice any of these, contact us for an inspection in Johnson City.
