    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Johnson City, TN

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater.     Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Johnson City

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Johnson City home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Johnson City

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Johnson City, TN

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Johnson City, TN

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.
