    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Johnson City, TN

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company.     Gas Leak Detection in Johnson City

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. The slow ones are where the gauge earns its keep. We isolate, then sniff. Testing happens with the appliance valves shut and again with them open, since some leaks hide past a valve. Finding the exact joint keeps the repair small.

    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Johnson City

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Johnson City the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered. The permit and the inspection booking are on us. Most inspections take under twenty minutes.     Gas Line Repair and Replacement in Johnson City, TN

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a pressure test.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. Rerouting with CSST past a leak in a finished wall is often cheaper than cutting and patching drywall. Nothing is turned back on until the test gauge holds steady.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Johnson City

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it.     Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Johnson City, TN

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.
