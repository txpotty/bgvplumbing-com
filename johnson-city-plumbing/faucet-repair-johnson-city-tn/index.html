    Commercial Faucet Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house.     Bathroom Faucet Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Johnson City, TN

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Look at the floor of the sink cabinet. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Johnson City

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Johnson City

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. That waste lands on your water bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Johnson City

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.
