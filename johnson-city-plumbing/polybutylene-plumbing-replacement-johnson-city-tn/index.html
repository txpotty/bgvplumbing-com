    Why Polybutylene Fails in Johnson City

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
