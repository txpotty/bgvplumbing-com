    What Our Tankless Plumbers Fix in Johnson City, TN

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Noritz heaters hold up well, though flow sensors and vent joints still fail with age. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Johnson City

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. We check element resistance and supply voltage. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Johnson City

    Water dripping from inside the cabinet, or a fault you cannot reset, should be seen that day. We bring sensors, igniters, flame rods and filters, so a good share of visits end with hot water. Heat exchangers are different. We check the warranty, order the part, and shut the water at the service valves so the cabinet stays dry until it arrives.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Johnson City, TN

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, while a sensor or igniter repair tends to run $250 to $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor. A new unit costs a lot more. Installed, a new gas tankless typically runs $3,000 to $5,500, so on a unit past 15 years with a failed exchanger we usually lean toward replacing.

    Commercial Tankless Repair in Johnson City, TN

    Hotels and gyms draw hot water from open to close, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit. We service rack systems with manifold kits, checking each heater's flow sensor and fault log separately, and set a descaling schedule by run hours. Quarterly is common.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Johnson City

    Restaurants and laundromats in Johnson City often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. One unit down means the others work harder and scale faster, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. A down kitchen loses money. We schedule around service hours when we can.
