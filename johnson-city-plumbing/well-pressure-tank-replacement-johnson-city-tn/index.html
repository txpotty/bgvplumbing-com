    Replacing Failed Well Tanks in Johnson City, TN

    BGV Plumbing replaces well pressure tanks, the steel tank that holds water under air pressure between the pump and your faucets. Inside is a rubber bladder or diaphragm that separates air from water. Once that bladder tears, the tank fills with water, the air cushion disappears, and the pump starts and stops every few seconds. Pumps hate that.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Johnson City, TN

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Johnson City, TN

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Johnson City, TN

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Johnson City, TN

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. Common sizes are easy for us to get. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Emergency Pressure Tank Replacement in Johnson City, TN

    If the pump is running nonstop or the taps are dry, call (615) 518-0893. A ruptured tank or a stuck switch can burn out a pump, and we work to get you running before the pump is damaged. Turn off the breaker first.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Johnson City

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. The connection height and size usually match. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Johnson City

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, which wears the pump and the switch contacts. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.
