    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Johnson City

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can crack buried pipes over time. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Backups in Johnson City

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. One slow sink by itself is usually just the trap or a short branch line, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Johnson City, TN

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. We also replace main shutoffs that will not turn.

    Water Heater Service and Installation in Johnson City, TN

    Tank or tankless, the failures repeat. A tank heater starts leaking at the bottom or rumbling from sediment, and tankless units throw error codes when scale builds up in the heat exchanger or the venting is wrong. We flush, descale and repair both types.
