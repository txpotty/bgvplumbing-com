    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Cleveland

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened.
