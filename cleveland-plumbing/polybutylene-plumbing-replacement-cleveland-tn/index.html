    Why Polybutylene Fails in Cleveland

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have moderately hard water. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
