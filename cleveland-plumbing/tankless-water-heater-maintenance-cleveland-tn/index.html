    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Cleveland

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring particular maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Cleveland homes.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Cleveland, TN

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Service in Cleveland, TN

    Even with regular maintenance, unexpected issues can arise with tankless water heaters. If you experience a sudden loss of hot water, error codes or unusual noises from your unit in Cleveland, TN, BGV Plumbing offers emergency services. We focus on rapid response to diagnose and resolve critical problems, often related to clogged heat exchangers or faulty sensors, restoring your hot water supply quickly and safely.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Cleveland

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Cleveland, TN

    Noritz tankless water heaters are sturdy systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, ensuring proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for the best efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Cleveland, TN

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing anything.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Cleveland

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.
