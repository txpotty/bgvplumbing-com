    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Cleveland, TN

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Families with small kids often like the trade.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Cleveland

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Cleveland

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. The mount goes on, the unit gets hung and the discharge tube is connected to a new trap, with the wall stub lowered if it sits too high. An electrician adds power if needed.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Cleveland, TN

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Garbage Disposal Installation Cost Factors in Cleveland, TN

    Most of the price comes from the unit and the electrical setup. A plain swap with power already in place costs the least, and new wiring, a lowered drain or a reworked double bowl each add labor as a separate line. The quote covers unit and labor.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Cleveland, TN

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age.     Air Switch Disposal Installation in Cleveland, TN

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.
