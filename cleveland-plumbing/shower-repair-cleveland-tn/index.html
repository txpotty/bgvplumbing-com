    Shower Repairs We Handle in Cleveland, TN

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Cleveland

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Cleveland, TN

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Cleveland

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. New valves include pressure balancing. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Cleveland, TN

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Cleveland, TN

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Cleveland, TN

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Next, call us. BGV Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Commercial Shower Service in Cleveland

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. After-hours work is available. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Cleveland

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.
