    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Cleveland

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Cleveland can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Cleveland

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have moderately hard water. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Cleveland, TN

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
