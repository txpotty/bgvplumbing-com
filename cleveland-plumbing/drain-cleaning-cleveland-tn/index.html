    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Cleveland

    Most single fixture drain cleanings in Cleveland run about $125 to $275. Main line cleaning from a cleanout typically costs $175 to $450, and adding a camera inspection usually adds $150 to $350.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often useless against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Tree roots are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing serious blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Most standard drain cleaning for a single fixture can be done within 1 to 2 hours. More extensive jobs, such as main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the difficulty of the clog.

    A plunger or a small hand auger can handle a simple hair clog near the drain. For deep clogs, main line backups or repeat problems, a pro has the machines and camera to find and fix the real cause.

    Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Mineral scale inside pipes leaves a rough surface that catches grease, soap and hair. Roots also push into sewer lines looking for water, especially during dry stretches.

    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more thorough cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.
