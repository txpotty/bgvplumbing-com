    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Cleveland

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Cleveland

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Cleveland, TN

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Tennessee ordinances for waste management.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Cleveland

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. BGV Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Cleveland for the best efficiency.
