    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Cleveland

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a seal kit and a new piston, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Cleveland, TN

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Cleveland

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Cleveland

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. The inlet and outlet piping, bypass, drain line and brine overflow all get traced and checked. Green crust on copper fittings, a slow leak at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Cleveland, TN

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Cleveland

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. Plan on new media every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.
