    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Cleveland, TN

    Many older homes in Cleveland, TN still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, clear water throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Cleveland

    Slab leaks, where pipes under your concrete foundation leak, are a significant concern for homeowners in Cleveland. These leaks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and wasted water. BGV Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to pinpoint the exact location of the leak. Depending on the severity and accessibility, we offer targeted pipe repair or rerouting and replacement solutions to permanently resolve the issue, protecting your home's structural integrity.

    PEX Repiping in Cleveland

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Cleveland, TN

    Copper piping is a long-standing, proven choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Cleveland, TN

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Cleveland, TN

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace accessible sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.
