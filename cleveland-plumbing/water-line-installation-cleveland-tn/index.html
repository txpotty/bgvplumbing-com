    Over time, main water lines can corrode, crack or leak because of shifting soil, tree root intrusion or age. BGV Plumbing focuses on replacing old, failing main water lines, often upgrading from galvanized steel or polybutylene to more durable PEX or copper.     Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Cleveland, TN

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the culprit. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is often paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Cleveland, TN

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and holds up against cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    Insulating Water Lines for Tennessee Weather in Cleveland, TN

    Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. Even where winters are mild, a hard freeze can still be a threat to exposed water lines. BGV Plumbing offers water line installation with proper insulation, especially for lines running through unheated spaces or near exterior walls in Cleveland, TN. This preventive step helps protect your pipes from freezing and bursting, safeguarding your home against costly water damage during cold snaps. We recommend insulation materials based on local conditions.
