    Choosing Drain Material in Cleveland, TN

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Drain Replacement vs Repair Costs in Cleveland, TN

    A single washer is cheaper today. On a drain past fifteen years old, the next joint often goes soon, and repeat calls cost more than one replacement. You see both options in writing. Your call.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Cleveland, TN

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Cleveland

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. They all get set together. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Cleveland

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    P-Trap Replacement in Cleveland

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. The new trap gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Cleveland, TN

    Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Forcing a scaled lift rod usually snaps the pivot and leaves the stopper stuck, with a narrowed tailpiece slowing the drain. We replace the whole scaled assembly. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.
