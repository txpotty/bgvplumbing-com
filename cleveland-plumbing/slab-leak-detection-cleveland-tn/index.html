    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Cleveland

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Cleveland

    We provide complete slab leak detection services tailored for residential properties throughout Cleveland. From single-family homes to townhouses, we understand the common plumbing systems and foundation types found in the area. Our goal is to quickly find the leak, provide a clear explanation and offer effective repair solutions to protect your home and peace of mind.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Cleveland, TN

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Cleveland

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Cleveland, TN

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Cleveland, TN home. We have separate methods for each.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Cleveland, TN

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Cleveland home.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Cleveland

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Cleveland homes.
