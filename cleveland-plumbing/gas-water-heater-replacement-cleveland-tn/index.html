    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Cleveland, TN

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Cleveland

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon gas tank usually runs $1,800 to $3,200 installed, haul-away and basic code upgrades included. Power vent and 75 gallon tanks run $3,000 to $4,800. We give a firm price first.

    Bradford White is our most common tank, with Rheem and Noritz also on the list. Your budget and daily hot water use decide which one we recommend. All three are well supported.

    Rusty hot water and rumbling from the tank are the usual early signs. Pilot outages, a puddle at the base or an age past 10 to 12 years all point to replacement soon. A leaking tank is done.

    Annual flushing and an anode check every few years are the main things. Most gas tanks die from sediment, which sits on the burner side and cooks the steel until it fails. A pro inspection helps too.

    Mostly Rheem, State and Bradford White. All three have solid warranties.

    Gas and venting mistakes are the dangerous part. A loose flare fitting leaks gas, and a vent connector without upward slope can spill carbon monoxide into the house while everything looks normal. Hire a licensed plumber.

    Flush the tank once a year. A yearly flush keeps sediment from hardening, and an anode check every three years catches a spent rod before the tank rusts. A right-sized tank lasts longer.

    Tank warranties usually run 6 to 10 years, with 1 to 2 years on parts like the gas valve and igniter. We add a labor warranty. Hang on to your paperwork.
