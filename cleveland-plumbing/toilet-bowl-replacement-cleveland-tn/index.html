    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Cleveland

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not. Some leak slowly, some all at once. The tank gets drained and lifted off, then the bowl comes out, and the matching bowl goes in on a fresh seal with new tank gaskets and bolts.

    Emergency Bowl Replacement in Cleveland

    Shut the angle stop behind the toilet the moment you see water coming from a cracked bowl, then pull towels around the base to slow the spread. Then call us.     Old Toilet Upgrades in Cleveland, TN

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume. Bowl and tank have to match. Upgrading an old toilet usually means replacing both, and we will price it that way.

    Restaurant and Gym Bowl Replacement in Cleveland, TN

    Gyms and restaurants break more bowls than offices do, usually at the rim or the bolt holes, and a broken bowl there means a restroom out of service. Work gets scheduled around your hours. Wall-hung bowls get a carrier check, since a loose carrier cracks the next bowl too.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Cleveland

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Some bowls are not sold on their own. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Cleveland

    Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. Water left sitting in a toilet in an unheated cabin or vacant house can freeze, and the ice cracks the bowl at the trap where you cannot see it. Drain them before winter. After replacing the bowl, we show you how to winterize it, which means shutting the stop, flushing and adding RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Cleveland

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. The bolt holes get a close look before we quote. A gasket and bolt kit fixes the first case, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.
