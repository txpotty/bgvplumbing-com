    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Cleveland, TN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Cleveland, TN

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Humidity promotes condensation and corrosion on cold water lines and fittings in crawlspaces, which is worth checking during routine service. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Cleveland

    Fast growth around Cleveland means many newer subdivisions, but older neighborhoods still depend on aging clay or cast iron sewer laterals. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Cleveland

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. Backfill goes in last.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Cleveland

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Cleveland

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Humidity encourages condensation and corrosion on cold water lines and fittings in crawlspaces, which is worth checking during routine service. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.
