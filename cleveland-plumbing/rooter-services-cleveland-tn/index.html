    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Cleveland, TN

    Kitchen lines clog slowly, over months, with grease, soap and food scraps that cool and harden on the pipe wall.     Video Camera Drain Inspection in Cleveland

    A camera on a push rod shows the inside of the pipe in real time. For a line that keeps plugging, it ends the guesswork fast. Roots at a joint, a lost toy, a cracked hub or a low spot holding water all show up clearly on screen. You can watch the screen alongside the tech.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Cleveland, TN

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. It strips grease, scale and root hair that a cable slides past. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. The pipe gets a camera check first. Pressure that high can damage cracked clay or thin cast iron, so we jet only after we have looked at the pipe.

    Tree Root Removal in Cleveland
