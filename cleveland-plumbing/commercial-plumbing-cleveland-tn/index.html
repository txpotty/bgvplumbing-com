    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in Cleveland, TN

    Commercial Leak Detection in Cleveland, TN

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Grease Trap Installation in Cleveland

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Cleveland

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Cleveland

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. Our work in multi-family buildings includes leak repair, heater swaps, stack clearing and full repipes, with posted notices before any shutoff. Managers get steady unit pricing. Invoices list each unit number, so your accounting team can bill back to the right account.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Cleveland

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. We also check trap primers.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Cleveland, TN

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless racks get descaled. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Cleveland

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. We can talk your staff through the shutoff while the plumber drives over, then isolate the broken branch and get the rest of the building back on water. Sewage backups get the same response. We clear the main, camera it and tell you whether it will happen again.
