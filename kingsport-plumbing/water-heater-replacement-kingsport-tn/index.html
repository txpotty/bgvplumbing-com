    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kingsport, TN

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Kingsport, TN homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Kingsport

    Businesses in Kingsport put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. We schedule around your hours.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Kingsport

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Kingsport

    Homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which have a history of failure and are a common repipe target. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.
