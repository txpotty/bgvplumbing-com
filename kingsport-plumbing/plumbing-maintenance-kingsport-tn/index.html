    Hot Water Tank Service in Kingsport, TN

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Kingsport

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Kingsport

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Kingsport

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. We repair failures. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.
