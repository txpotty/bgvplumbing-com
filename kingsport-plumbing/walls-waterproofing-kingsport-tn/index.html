    Wall Waterproofing Work We Handle in Kingsport, TN

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Kingsport

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Hot, humid summers and cool winters with frequent freeze and thaw cycles are the main climate stresses on plumbing in Tennessee. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Kingsport

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Kingsport

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Drainage System Integration in Kingsport, TN

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Kingsport

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Kingsport, TN

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Kingsport, TN

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Kingsport, TN

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.
