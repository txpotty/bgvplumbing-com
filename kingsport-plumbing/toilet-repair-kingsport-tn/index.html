    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Kingsport, TN

    Running Toilet Repair in Kingsport

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is almost always losing water past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Kingsport, TN

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A loose or cracked handle is a five-minute swap.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Kingsport, TN

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. When one tank seal has failed, we replace the full set so the others do not follow next month.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Kingsport

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Kingsport, TN

    Shutting off the angle stop buys you time, but an old multi-turn stop valve can crumble or leak at the stem when it has not been moved in fifteen years. Do not force it. If it will not close, the main shutoff for the house is the backup, and we can replace the stop with a quarter-turn valve once the water is off.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Kingsport

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The water level drops until the fill valve kicks on again. Sometimes the cause is a refill tube pushed too far down the overflow, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Kingsport, TN

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot. With a slab floor, the flange is often anchored into concrete and can crack if the closet bolts are overtightened. We check it. If the flange is cracked, we add a stainless repair ring or replace it, then reset the toilet on a new seal.
