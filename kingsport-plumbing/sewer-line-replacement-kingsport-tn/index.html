    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Kingsport, TN

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Kingsport, TN

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Kingsport, TN

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem.     Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Kingsport, TN

    A belly is a low spot that holds water.     Sewer Permits and Code in Kingsport, TN
