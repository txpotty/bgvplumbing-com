    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Kingsport

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Kingsport, TN

    Crack Injection for Basements in Kingsport

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy bonds the two sides. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.
