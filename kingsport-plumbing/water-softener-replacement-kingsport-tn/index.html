    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Kingsport, TN

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Kingsport

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Whole-Home Water Softener System Upgrades in Kingsport

    Upgrading your whole-home water softener system in Kingsport is a significant investment that pays off in the long run. Older systems may struggle with the mineral content in your water or simply be undersized for your household's needs.
