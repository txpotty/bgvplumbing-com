    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Kingsport

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Kingsport, TN

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Kingsport, TN

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already.     Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Kingsport

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Kingsport, TN
