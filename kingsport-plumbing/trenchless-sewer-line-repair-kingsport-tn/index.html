    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Kingsport

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Kingsport homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Kingsport, TN

    Pipe Bursting in Kingsport, TN

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.
