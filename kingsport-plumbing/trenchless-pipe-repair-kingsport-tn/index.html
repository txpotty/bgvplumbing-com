    Pipe Bursting in Kingsport, TN

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Most cracked, rooted or separated pipes can be lined. Pipes that are fully collapsed or badly bellied may need bursting or traditional repair. A camera inspection tells us which method works best.

    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, which leaves you with a completely new pipe.

    Trenchless pipe repairs, especially CIPP lining, are designed to be extremely durable and long-lasting.
