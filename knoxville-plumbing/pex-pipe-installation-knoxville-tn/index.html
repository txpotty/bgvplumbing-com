    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Knoxville

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Knoxville

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Knoxville, TN

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Knoxville, TN are good candidates for PEX as much as houses can. With a manifold and shutoff for each unit, a leak in one apartment only takes that apartment offline, not the whole riser. Rated walls need firestopping. Every penetration through a fire-rated wall gets a listed firestop sleeve or sealant.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Knoxville, TN

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Knoxville, TN

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Drywall patching may be separate. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Knoxville

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.
