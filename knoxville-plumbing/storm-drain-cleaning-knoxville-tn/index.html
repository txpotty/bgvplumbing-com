    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris.     Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Knoxville

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Storm Drain Repair vs. Cleaning Costs in Knoxville, TN

    Understanding whether you need storm drain cleaning or a more extensive repair in Knoxville, TN can save you money. Cleaning typically costs between $200 and $600, addressing simple blockages.     Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Knoxville, TN

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to confirm flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.
