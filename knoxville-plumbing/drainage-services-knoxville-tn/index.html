    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Knoxville

    Cost depends on whether it is a clog or a construction job. A drain clearing usually stays under a few hundred dollars. Camera inspections, jetting and sewer repairs step up from there, and yard systems like French drains, channel drains or dry wells are priced by the length of drain and the amount of digging involved. We quote in writing first.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. Clean them when they slow down. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    They solve different problems. A French drain collects water that soaks into the soil. A channel drain catches surface water sheeting across concrete, like a driveway that slopes toward the garage, and many yards need one of each because the water arrives both ways during a hard storm.

    We recommend skipping them. Harsh chemicals can damage older pipes. They do not remove scale or roots, and leftover cleaner in the trap is a splash hazard.

    A single fixture drain usually takes about an hour. Plan on one to two hours for a main line. Camera work adds 30 to 60 minutes, and a buried or missing cleanout adds more.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. Those point to the main line. Outside, call when water stands in the yard for more than a day after rain, when a downspout overflows the gutter in every storm, or when water shows up in the crawlspace or basement after a heavy rain.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. A main line needs more time. With a camera inspection and a clean, accessible cleanout, a main line job usually wraps up in about two hours, though roots or a hard-to-reach cleanout can stretch it to most of a morning.

    Drain clearing carries a service guarantee for a set period. If the line clogs again in that window, we come back. If the camera shows broken or root-filled pipe, we tell you the clearing is temporary.
