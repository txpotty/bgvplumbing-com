    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Knoxville, TN

    While less common, some large residential kitchens or homes with extensive outdoor cooking setups may benefit from a small grease interceptor. These are typically installed under the sink and help prevent fats, oils, and grease from clogging household drains. BGV Plumbing can assess your home's needs and provide a suitable residential grease trap installation solution that protects your plumbing system from FOG accumulation.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Knoxville, TN

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Knoxville, TN. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for reliable performance and automated FOG removal.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Knoxville, TN

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Tennessee ordinances for waste management.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Knoxville, TN

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are usually a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection. That way there are no surprises during construction.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Knoxville

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Knoxville. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experts in installing gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to maximize their efficiency.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Knoxville, TN

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks usually range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can crack buried pipes over time. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go far longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Knoxville, TN

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an unnecessary expense. Our plumbers carefully calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, delivering optimal performance for your Knoxville, TN business.
