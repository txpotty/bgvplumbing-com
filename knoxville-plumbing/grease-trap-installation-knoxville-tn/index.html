    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Install in Knoxville, TN

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Knoxville

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. The lid gasket is the usual leak point after a few years. We keep spares on the truck. Most Zurn units come with a flow control fitting, and we mount it on the inlet side at the height the instructions call for.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Knoxville

    On a new build-out, the grease trap gets decided before the slab is poured, not after. The drawings and the equipment list from your kitchen designer tell us what will drain where. Changes get expensive. Saw-cutting a finished floor to relocate a trap can add several days to a build-out, so we flag sizing or location problems at plan review.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Knoxville, TN

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Knoxville, TN

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Concrete is heavier and holds up under traffic. Poly is easier to set. With a plastic tank in wet ground we add anti-flotation anchoring, because an empty tank in saturated soil can pop right out of the hole.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Knoxville

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in Knoxville, TN

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. A failed inspection can bring a fine or an order to upgrade. We install to pass. That means a sample port where the program wants one and a unit sized to the number their formula gives, not the number the last contractor guessed.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Knoxville

    School cafeterias and hospital kitchens cook in big batches and wash in big batches, which means large pot sinks and a dish machine running hard for two hours after each meal. Most end up with an outdoor gravity interceptor. We schedule around the calendar. School jobs fit into breaks, and hospital jobs get phased so meals keep going out.
