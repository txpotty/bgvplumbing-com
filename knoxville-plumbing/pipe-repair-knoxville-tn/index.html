    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Knoxville

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. Repairs are possible. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. The schedule is yours.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Knoxville

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Knoxville, TN

    A repair starts with finding the leak. Wrong guesses cost money. We use electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and moisture meters, shutting off zones one at a time until the leak has nowhere to hide. Then we make one opening.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Knoxville

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Knoxville

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Usually a quick fix.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Knoxville, TN

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper gets pinholes. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, so the truck carries tools and fittings for both. Each repair is pressure tested.
