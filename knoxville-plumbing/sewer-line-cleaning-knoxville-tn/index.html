    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Knoxville

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. It narrows the line over time. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Line Cleaning and Inspection in Knoxville, TN
