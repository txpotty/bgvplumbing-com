    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Knoxville

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides crucial protection when the power goes out. We install dependable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an important safeguard for homes in Knoxville that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We tie these systems cleanly with your existing plumbing.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Knoxville

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually disconnect hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Sump Pump Pit Installation in Knoxville, TN

    A properly built sump pit is fundamental to an effective sump pump system. If your basement lacks a suitable pit or needs an upgrade, BGV Plumbing provides professional sump pit installation. We carefully excavate and prepare the pit to the correct dimensions and depth for proper drainage and pump function. This first step is critical for any new sump pump installation and prevents the sediment buildup that can damage the pump.
