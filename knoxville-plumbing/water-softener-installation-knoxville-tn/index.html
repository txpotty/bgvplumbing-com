    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Knoxville

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Knoxville, TN

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Knoxville

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Knoxville, TN

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Filters add cost. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Knoxville

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Knoxville, TN

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.

    Water Softener Replacement in Knoxville, TN

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Softener Loop Installation in Knoxville

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.
