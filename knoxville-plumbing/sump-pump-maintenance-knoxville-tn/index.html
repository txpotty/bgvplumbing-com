    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Knoxville

    BGV Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability.     Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Knoxville

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Knoxville, TN

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Knoxville

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Knoxville

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Knoxville, TN

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Knoxville, TN.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Knoxville

    Strong storm systems can cut power for hours, which is when a battery backup sump pump earns its keep in a basement home. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Knoxville, TN

    Routine maintenance typically costs a fraction of replacing a burned-out pump and cleaning up a flooded basement. A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. We will tell you honestly whether yours has more years left or is ready to retire.
