    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Knoxville

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Knoxville, TN

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more common in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Knoxville, TN

    When repairs are no longer sufficient due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer several replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive solution for your Tennessee property, giving you a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Knoxville, TN

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Knoxville neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe integrity. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Knoxville

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-English summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.
