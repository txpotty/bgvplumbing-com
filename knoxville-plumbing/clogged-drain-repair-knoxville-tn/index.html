    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Knoxville

    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Knoxville

    The cost of clogged drain repair in Knoxville, TN can vary widely, usually ranging from $150 to $500 for standard clogs cleared with snaking or augering. More complex issues like main sewer line blockages requiring hydro-jetting or excavation could range from $500 to $2,000 or more. We provide clear, upfront pricing after diagnosis.

    Most standard clogged drain repairs, such as those in a sink or bathtub, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues, like main sewer line clogs or those requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the difficulty.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear stubborn blockages and completely clean the inside of your pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs caused by tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or significant mineral deposits from hard water, which traditional snaking might not fully clear.

    Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have fairly hard water. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside. Both conditions make clogs form faster.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help limit mineral buildup.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, BGV Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Knoxville. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.

    Gurgling sounds, water backing up into a different fixture and frequent clogs in several drains point to a deeper issue. Sewage odors or water around a floor drain are also warning signs. These often mean the main line needs attention.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch finishes.
