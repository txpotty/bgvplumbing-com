    Mineral Scale and Sludge Removal in Knoxville

    Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have moderately hard water. Over time, minerals in the water can deposit as hard scale on the inside of pipes, narrowing the passage and trapping debris. Sludge from detergents, soaps, and organic matter also adds to blockages. Our drain jetting service blasts away these hard mineral deposits and thick sludge layers, restoring the full diameter of your pipes and improving drainage in your Knoxville home or business.

    Kitchen Drain Jetting in Knoxville

    Kitchen lines are where grease, food particles and dish soap collect and harden. Over time the pipe narrows until the sink drains slowly or backs up into the other bowl. We access the line through a cleanout or the trap arm and feed a small jetting hose toward the main. Rear-facing jets scrub the walls while the stream flushes residue away. Your sink and dishwasher drain at full speed again.

    Laundry Drain Jetting in Knoxville, TN

    Washing machines discharge lint, fabric softener residue and fine sand or grit. These build into a gray sludge that slows the standpipe and spills over during the spin cycle. Jetting clears the standpipe and branch line completely. We also check the trap, vent and drain size to confirm they can handle the washer flow. A lint filter on the discharge hose helps keep the line clear.

    Storm Drain Jetting in Knoxville

    Storm drains are essential for managing rainwater runoff. Heavy spring and fall rains can cause flash flooding in Tennessee, and saturated ground often leads to wet crawlspaces and sewer backups. Over time, these drains can clog with leaves, silt, debris, and trash, leading to local flooding. Our storm drain jetting service in Knoxville clears these blockages, keeping water moving and preventing water damage to your property. We use high-volume jetting equipment that moves large amounts of debris to restore your storm drainage system.

    Bathroom Branch Line Jetting in Knoxville

    Bathroom drains collect hair, soap scum and mineral scale from hard water. When several bathroom fixtures drain slowly together, the shared branch line may be coated. We jet the branch to remove buildup that a hand auger cannot reach. This helps restore drainage to sinks, tubs and showers on the same line. We test each fixture before we leave.

    Root Removal with Jetting in Knoxville, TN

    Tree roots are a frequent problem for sewer lines in Knoxville, TN, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots can get into tiny cracks in pipes, growing and over time causing complete blockages. Our drain jetting service includes root-cutting nozzles that slice through even thick root masses, flushing them out of your system. We can then inspect the line with a camera to check for any pipe damage and recommend further repairs if needed.

    Drain Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Knoxville

    Hard water leaves mineral deposits inside drain pipes, especially in older galvanized and cast iron. That rough scale traps hair, grease and soap, leading to frequent clogs. A jetting nozzle designed for scale can break up and wash away much of this buildup. We use the camera to judge how much wall remains before and after. Clean pipe drains better and clogs less frequently.
