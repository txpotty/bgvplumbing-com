    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Knoxville, TN

    Leaks in underground water lines, often leading from the meter to your home, can result in serious water loss and property damage. We use ground microphones and tracer gas detection to locate these buried leaks precisely.     Main Water Line Leak Detection in Knoxville

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, unusually green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, focused hole.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Knoxville, TN

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to serious drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to accurately pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's walls and ceilings.
