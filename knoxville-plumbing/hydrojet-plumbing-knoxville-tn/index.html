    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Knoxville

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Knoxville

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. On-call trucks are fully equipped. That means we can clear and jet a grease-packed restaurant line or a root-filled home sewer in one visit, without waiting until Monday to bring the jetter back for a second trip.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Knoxville

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Knoxville, TN

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Root Removal Jetting in Knoxville

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Roots come back through the same cracks, so the camera shows where a repair or liner would stop them.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Knoxville, TN

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Knoxville

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.
