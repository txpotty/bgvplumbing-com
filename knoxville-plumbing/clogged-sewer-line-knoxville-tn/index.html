    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Knoxville

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Roots love those joints. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, so we cut them with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then run a camera to see how much damage remains. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Knoxville, TN

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. You see the footage first.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Knoxville

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. That makes every clog harder. Without one, a plumber has to pull a toilet or climb on the roof to reach the main, so we install a two-way cleanout near the foundation that gives direct access upstream and downstream. It pays for itself on the next call.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Knoxville

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Commercial Clogged Sewer Line Service in Knoxville

    Commercial sewer lines in Knoxville carry far more than a house does. Grease is the main enemy. BGV Plumbing clears restaurant mains, apartment laterals and office drains with heavy equipment, and we can set up a jetting schedule to keep the line open between grease trap pumpings.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Knoxville, TN

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. The pipe gets cleaned all the way around. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Knoxville
