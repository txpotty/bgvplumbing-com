    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Knoxville, TN

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Knoxville, TN. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

It is an excellent preventative maintenance tool and is particularly effective for older pipes in Knoxville that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.
