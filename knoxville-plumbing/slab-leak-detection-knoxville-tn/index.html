    How We Find Slab Leaks in Knoxville, TN

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Knoxville, TN

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Knoxville, TN

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile needs attention the same day. Close the main first. BGV Plumbing sends a tech to Knoxville, TN homes quickly, isolating the leak so you have water elsewhere while we plan the repair.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Knoxville, TN

    Older homes in Knoxville, TN often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. It lets us test the most likely spots first.

    Commercial Slab Leak Detection in Knoxville, TN

    Offices, shops and restaurants on slabs in Knoxville, TN can lose a lot of water fast because their supply lines are larger. We test after hours if needed. BGV Plumbing isolates zones, traces mains and branch lines under the floor, then marks the leak so the repair fits around your hours.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Knoxville

    Water flooding a hallway or seeping through grout needs fast attention. Shut the main. We respond quickly, isolate the leak and usually get water back to the rest of the home that day, then come back to locate and repair the line properly.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Knoxville

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Knoxville

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Knoxville, TN

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, repair the section with approved fittings and pressure test it. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. Accurate marks keep that hole small.
