    Tankless Water Heater Service in Knoxville, TN

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Knoxville, TN

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. High demand speeds up scale and wear, so commercial units need service on a tighter schedule. We service them regularly. We keep your business supplied.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Knoxville

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Anode Rod Checks in Knoxville

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Knoxville, TN

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Knoxville

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. Efficiency comes back. Recovery speeds up.

    Tankless Descaling in Knoxville, TN

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. We clean the inlet filter. We also check the burner and venting.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Knoxville

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. Pans get inspected. We check venting and access.
