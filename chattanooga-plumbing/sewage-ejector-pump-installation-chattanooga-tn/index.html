    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Chattanooga

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. BGV Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Chattanooga. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Chattanooga, TN

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and homes with heavy below-grade use often need duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. If one pump fails, the other keeps working while an alarm notifies staff. We size the basin and pumps for peak flow and install controls to code. Maintenance schedules are provided to keep both pumps in good shape.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Chattanooga

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet and pumps waste through small-diameter pipe up to the drain line. They work well for small basement bathrooms, laundry rooms and guest suites.     Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Chattanooga

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so toilets, showers and sinks drain into a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We excavate the floor, set a basin usually 30 to 50 gallons and connect each fixture drain. A solids-handling pump lifts waste up and into the main drain line. The basin lid is sealed and vented to keep odors from escaping.
