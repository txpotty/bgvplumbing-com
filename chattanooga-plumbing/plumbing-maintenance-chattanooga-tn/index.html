    Backflow Preventer Testing in Chattanooga

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Chattanooga, TN

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still wise. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually disconnect hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Chattanooga

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Chattanooga, TN

    Strong storm systems can knock out power for hours, which is when a battery backup sump pump earns its keep in a basement home. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Chattanooga.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Chattanooga

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups.     Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Chattanooga

    Fast growth around Chattanooga means many newer subdivisions, but older neighborhoods still rely on aging clay or cast iron sewer laterals. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Chattanooga, TN

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Chattanooga

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.
