    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Chattanooga, TN

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Expect a conversion to take a full day.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Chattanooga, TN

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Chattanooga, TN

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use in check while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is popular in long single-story ranch homes.
