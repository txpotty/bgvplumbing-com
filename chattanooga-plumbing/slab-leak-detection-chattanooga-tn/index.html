    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Chattanooga, TN

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Chattanooga.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Chattanooga, TN

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We prioritize non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Chattanooga, TN from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and lowers the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Chattanooga

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Chattanooga homes.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Chattanooga, TN

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Chattanooga

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.
