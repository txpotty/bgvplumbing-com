    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Chattanooga, TN

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Chattanooga

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Chattanooga, TN home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Chattanooga
