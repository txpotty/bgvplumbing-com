    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Chattanooga

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga

    Gas tankless units in Chattanooga are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga

    Propane homes in Chattanooga are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. Tank size matters too.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Chattanooga, TN

    Upfront, a tankless install in Chattanooga, TN costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. Ask for both prices.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Chattanooga, TN

    Businesses in Chattanooga, TN with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. We size the rack by peak demand. Expect a bigger gas line and a mechanical permit on commercial jobs.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Chattanooga

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. We weigh both. A far bath may need recirc.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga

    Commercial hot water loads in Chattanooga run longer and harder than a house. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. We rack units with a common manifold. One can be serviced while the rest run.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga, TN

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and replacement parts are widely stocked. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.
