    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Less run time, less wear.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Chattanooga

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Chattanooga, TN

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, so we look over the pipe you can see and test the pressure. A regulator often helps.
