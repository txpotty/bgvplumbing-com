    What We Replace on Well Systems in Chattanooga, TN

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Chattanooga

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. We check it on every pump job.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Chattanooga

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Parts and control boxes are easy to find. We match the motor to the pump end and the wire size to the run length.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Chattanooga, TN

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Chattanooga

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. The soft start is easier on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.

    No-Water Calls in Chattanooga

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. We test the control box and pressure switch before pulling anything.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Chattanooga

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.
