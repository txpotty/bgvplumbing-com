    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Chattanooga

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels often need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Chattanooga

    Power outages during heavy storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides vital protection when the power goes out. We install reliable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an essential safeguard for homes in Chattanooga that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We integrate these systems cleanly with your existing plumbing.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Chattanooga

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Chattanooga, TN

    A high water alarm is an affordable part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor. We install standalone alarms or Wi-Fi monitors that send a text if the water level rises, the pump fails or power drops. These are popular with homeowners who travel or own second homes. We test the float trigger in front of you so you know how the alarm sounds.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Chattanooga

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water effectively. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Chattanooga property.
