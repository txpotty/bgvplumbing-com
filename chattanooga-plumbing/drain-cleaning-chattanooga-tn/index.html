    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Chattanooga

    Most single fixture drain cleanings in Chattanooga run about $125 to $275. Main line cleaning from a cleanout typically costs $175 to $450, and adding a camera inspection usually adds $150 to $350.

    A plunger or a small hand auger can handle a simple hair clog near the drain. For deep clogs, main line backups or repeat problems, a pro has the machines and camera to find and correct the real cause.

    Hydro jetting is generally safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old Chattanooga properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to assess pipe condition and use the safest method.

    Hydro jetting is recommended for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    Tree roots are attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing serious blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also rarely clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is safer and more effective.

    Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Mineral scale inside pipes leaves a rough surface that catches grease, soap and hair. Roots also push into sewer lines looking for water, especially during dry stretches.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens to trap hair and food particles, flush only toilet paper, and consider periodic professional drain maintenance, particularly if you have older pipes or large trees nearby.
