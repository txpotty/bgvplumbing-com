    Choosing a Backup Battery in Chattanooga

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Chattanooga

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Chattanooga

    Creeks and rivers rise quickly after a heavy rain, so homes in low areas benefit from backwater valves and good yard drainage. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Chattanooga

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Takes about two hours.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. After that, we look at why the old one quit.
