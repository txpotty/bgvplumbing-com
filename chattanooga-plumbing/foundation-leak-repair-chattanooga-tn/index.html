    Foundation Leak Detection in Chattanooga

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Water Line Reroute in Chattanooga

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Chattanooga, TN

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. BGV Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Chattanooga, TN home and ensuring a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Chattanooga, TN

Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned line. A full repipe is the most comprehensive option for homes with multiple leaks or aging pipe.
