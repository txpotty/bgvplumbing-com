    PEX Repiping in Chattanooga

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually unhook garden hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Chattanooga, TN

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Chattanooga, TN

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main.     Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Chattanooga

    Private wells serve many rural homes in Tennessee, and some well water carries iron, sulfur or sediment that calls for filtration or treatment.
