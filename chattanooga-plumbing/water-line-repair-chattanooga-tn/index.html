    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Chattanooga

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Chattanooga

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Chattanooga

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Fast growth around Chattanooga means many newer subdivisions, but older neighborhoods still rely on aging clay or cast iron sewer laterals. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.

    Underground Leak Detection in Chattanooga, TN

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Some leaks barely make a sound. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can break buried pipes over time. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.
