    Gas Leak Detection in Chattanooga

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose.     Cost of Gas Line Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Gas line repair prices in Chattanooga, TN depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Small fixes stay in the low hundreds.     Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Chattanooga

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Chattanooga, TN

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Chattanooga, TN

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Chattanooga, TN

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Chattanooga, TN. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. The permit is on us. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.
