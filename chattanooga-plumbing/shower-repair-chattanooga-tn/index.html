    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Chattanooga

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Chattanooga

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. We pull the trim and look. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Chattanooga

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Chattanooga

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
