    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Chattanooga, TN

    If the same drain clogs every few weeks, the problem is often a sagging pipe, a broken fitting or heavy scale. We run a small inspection camera through the line to see the inside of the pipe.     Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Chattanooga, TN

    Outdoor drains, including those for patios, driveways, and storm drains, are important for preventing water damage. We provide thorough cleaning and repair for outdoor drainage systems to ensure proper water runoff and protect your property's foundation in Chattanooga.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Chattanooga, TN

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we inspect both.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Chattanooga, TN

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces during the same visit.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Chattanooga, TN

    For severe or recurring clogs, hydro-jetting is an excellent solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away buildup, grease, scale, and even tree roots from your pipes. It's a complete cleaning process that leaves pipes scoured, reducing the likelihood of future blockages and extending pipe life, especially in homes with hard water deposits.
