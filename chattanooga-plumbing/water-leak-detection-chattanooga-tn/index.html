    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water on their own when they detect a leak.     Pinhole Leak Detection in Chattanooga, TN

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We employ non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity.     Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Chattanooga

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines.     Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Chattanooga

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter.
