    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Sink Installation in Chattanooga

    A basic drop-in kitchen sink install usually costs $300 to $600 for labor. Undermount and farmhouse installs usually run $450 to $1,200, and adding a disposal or relocating the drain adds $150 to $600.

    A like-for-like replacement usually does not. Moving drain lines or adding a new sink location may require a permit, depending on your local building department.

    We install Kohler, Blanco, Elkay, Kraus, Franke, Ruvati and many others. You can supply your sink or we can recommend one.

    We guarantee our labor, and sinks, faucets and disposals carry manufacturer warranties, many lifetime on the sink itself.

    While it's possible to attempt a DIY kitchen sink installation, it's usually not recommended unless you have significant plumbing experience. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and costly repairs down the line. Professional installation supports code compliance and long-term reliability.

    A simple drop-in swap is doable for experienced DIYers. Undermount, farmhouse and double-bowl installs with disposals are more complex and more likely to leak.

    Top-mount (drop-in) sinks sit on top of the counter, with a visible rim, and are generally easier to install. Under-mount sinks are installed beneath the counter, creating a seamless look, but require more complex sealing and support for a secure installation.

    Signs you might need a new kitchen sink include persistent leaks around the drain or edges, visible cracks or chips, severe staining that won't clean, rust spots, or a desire to upgrade your kitchen's look. If your current sink is old or damaged, replacement is often the smartest solution.
