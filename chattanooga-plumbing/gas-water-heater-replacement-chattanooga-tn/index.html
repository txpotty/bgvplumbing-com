    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Chattanooga, TN

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Chattanooga

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Chattanooga, TN

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Chattanooga

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Tennessee homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Chattanooga, TN

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Chattanooga

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Chattanooga, TN

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Tennessee plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.
