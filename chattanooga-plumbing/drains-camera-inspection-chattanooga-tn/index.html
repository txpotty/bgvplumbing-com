    Identifying Tree Root Intrusion in Chattanooga, TN

    Roots on camera look like fine hairs at first. Then they become a mat. Our footage shows where they come in, and whether it is light growth or a heavy mass. The answer changes the repair. Light roots need cutting, heavy ones need lining or replacement.

    Main Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Chattanooga

    A main line scope follows the pipe from the cleanout to the city tap or the septic tank. Every joint gets a look. The sonde in the camera head transmits a signal, letting us paint each problem spot and note the depth. The report is usable by anyone you hire.

    Housing in Tennessee ranges from older homes with cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines to newer builds plumbed with PEX. Drains under a slab cannot be seen.
