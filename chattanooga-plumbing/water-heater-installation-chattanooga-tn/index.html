    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. First step is to close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the gas or the breaker, which stops the leak from getting worse. We carry common sizes. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga, TN

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga, TN

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga, TN

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga, TN

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. Our crews know each one. We help you compare models on warranty length and recovery rate.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Chattanooga

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Chattanooga

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Chattanooga

    Water heater installation in Chattanooga, TN runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. The biggest factors are whether it is a tank or tankless unit, its size, its fuel and whether the gas line or panel needs work. Tankless costs more. You get a written quote after we see the space.

    Manufacturer warranties on the tank and parts usually run anywhere from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model and the brand you choose. BGV Plumbing adds a labor warranty on our work. Ask us for exact terms.

    Popping or rumbling, rust-colored water, leaks or an age past ten years are the common clues. One of those alone is reason enough to have it checked.

    Most jurisdictions require a permit for replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It keeps pressure spikes off your plumbing.

    Tank water heaters last about 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help.

    Permits are common for water heater work. Water heater replacements, sewer line work and repiping typically require a permit, and inspection habits can vary from county to county. BGV Plumbing takes care of permits and inspections in Chattanooga.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.
