    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Chattanooga

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so replacing the whole run is cheaper over five years. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Chattanooga

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. After a second leak in a new spot, we stop recommending patches. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, with fresh connections at the meter and at the shutoff. Most homes are done in a day.

    Commercial Water Line Replacement in Chattanooga

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. We handle the backflow test and certification paperwork. Trench shoring comes into play below five feet, and we plan the work so the outage lasts hours instead of days, often running a temporary supply while the new line is tied in. Night work is possible.

    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Chattanooga, TN

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Burst Main Line Response in Chattanooga, TN

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Chattanooga

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Chattanooga

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. No old line is needed to follow. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, and the launch and exit pits stay small. Heavy rock is the exception.
