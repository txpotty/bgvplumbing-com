    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. They clog the most. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct trap heights so the arm has the right fall to the wall. We also check the disposal and dishwasher connections.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Much of Tennessee sits on clay soils over limestone, and shallow rock can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slower and harder. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Chattanooga

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Undersized drains spill over. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, and for garage and utility floor drains we restore the trap seal so sewer odor stays in the pipe. Trap primers get repaired too.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Chattanooga

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe. We look at the disposal outlet too.

    Drain Vent Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe.     Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first.
