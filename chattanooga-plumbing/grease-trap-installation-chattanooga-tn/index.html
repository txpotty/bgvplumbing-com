    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Chattanooga

    The cost of grease trap installation in Chattanooga, TN varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    An indoor under-sink trap can often be installed in a day. Outdoor interceptors typically take several days to a week or more, depending on excavation, concrete work and inspection scheduling.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are typically rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    BGV Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will usually come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, ensuring peace of mind.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you determine the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are usually placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    Most food service businesses that cook, wash dishes or prepare food with fats, oils or grease are required to have one. Local pretreatment programs set the specific rules, and we can help you find out what applies to your site.

    Manufacturer warranties vary, with many polyethylene units carrying long structural warranties. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and connections.
