    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Chattanooga, TN

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten.     Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Chattanooga

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Chattanooga

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute.     Faucet Base Leak Repair in Chattanooga

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Chattanooga, TN

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Chattanooga, TN

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
