    The cleaning frequency depends heavily on your business's FOG output and the size of your trap. Most restaurants require cleaning every 1 to 3 months, while high-volume establishments might need it monthly. Local health codes in Chattanooga may also specify minimum frequencies.

    Neglecting commercial grease trap cleaning in Chattanooga can lead to severe drain blockages, sewage backups, foul odors impacting your business, and potential fines from the local health department or wastewater authority. It can also cause costly plumbing emergencies and business downtime.

    BGV Plumbing services all common types of grease traps and interceptors, including passive hydromechanical grease interceptors (HGIs), automatic grease removal units (AGRUs), and large gravity grease interceptors (GGIs). We handle both indoor under-sink units and outdoor in-ground tanks.

    FOG stands for Fats, Oils, and Grease. When poured down drains, FOG cools and solidifies, adhering to pipe walls and eventually leading to severe clogs. In grease traps, it separates from wastewater, but if not regularly removed, it overflows into the sewer system, causing blockages and environmental issues.

    Grease trap cleaning removes fats, oils, and grease (FOG), along with food solids, sediment, and wastewater. This material is often referred to as 'brown grease' and requires specialized handling and disposal to avoid environmental contamination.
