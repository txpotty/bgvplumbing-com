    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Collierville

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Collierville

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. BGV Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Collierville for maximum efficiency.
