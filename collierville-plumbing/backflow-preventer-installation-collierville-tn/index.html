    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Collierville

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Collierville, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.
