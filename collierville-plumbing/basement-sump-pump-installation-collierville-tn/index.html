    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Collierville

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes.     Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Collierville

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. Installing a new pit through the slab usually falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and adding a battery backup adds about $600 to $1,500.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, designed to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for the best protection.

    A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

    Strong storm systems can cut power for hours, which is when a battery backup sump pump earns its keep in a basement home. Because storms and power outages tend to arrive together, a battery backup sump pump is highly recommended. It offers crucial protection by switching on automatically when the main power fails, so your basement stays dry even without electricity. It is a smart investment for peace of mind.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the exact coverage for the model you choose.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump every 2 to 3 months, especially before and after the rainy season. Simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to make sure the pump activates, drains the water and shuts off properly. Regular testing helps catch issues early.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves complex plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing. Incorrect installation can lead to pump failure, electrical hazards or poor water removal, which can cause serious flood damage. It is always best to hire a licensed plumber for a correct, code-compliant installation in Collierville.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it has to be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.
