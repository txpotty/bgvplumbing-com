    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Collierville, TN

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Creeks and rivers rise quickly after a downpour, so homes in low areas benefit from backwater valves and good yard drainage. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Collierville

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Collierville homes. They are louder, though.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Collierville, TN

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Collierville, TN we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Collierville

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. We size the pump to your inflow and lift, not to the sale sticker. In Collierville, we set the basin at the low corner, bed it in washed gravel and run 1-1/2 inch PVC out through the rim joist. Discharge gets carried far enough out that it cannot soak back toward the wall.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Collierville

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid. A submersible with a gasketed cover keeps the noise down, and we route the discharge behind framing or through a closet so the pipe does not run across a wall you just painted in your Collierville home.
