    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Collierville

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options.     Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Collierville

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. Paperwork stays on file.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Collierville, TN

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead.
