    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Collierville, TN

    PEX rarely fails on its own. Most failures trace back to a nail or screw through the pipe, a rodent working on an attic run, or a fitting that was not crimped fully. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Collierville, TN come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Collierville

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.
