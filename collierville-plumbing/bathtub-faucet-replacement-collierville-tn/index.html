    Tub Spout Replacement in Collierville

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Collierville, TN

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Collierville

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Collierville

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.
