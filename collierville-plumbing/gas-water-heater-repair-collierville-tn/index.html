    Pilot Light Repair in Collierville

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Collierville, TN

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Collierville

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Collierville

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure.
