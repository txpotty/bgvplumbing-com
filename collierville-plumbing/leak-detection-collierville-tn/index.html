    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test.     Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Collierville

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab.
