    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Collierville, TN

    Many basements rely on a sump pit to handle storm runoff and groundwater. Pumps from brands like Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne fail most often from a stuck float or a burned-out motor after sitting idle for long stretches. We repair or replace the pump, check the check valve and route the discharge well away from the foundation. A battery backup is worth considering because big storms often knock out power.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Collierville

    Water lines that run along basement ceilings and walls can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Water chemistry affects how fast copper corrodes, and older galvanized lines frequently rust from the inside. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section with copper or PEX and pressure test the repair. If the line shows widespread wear, we'll tell you whether a partial repipe makes more sense.
