    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel.     Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Collierville

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a solid choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Collierville

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing work above certain thresholds requires a state licensed contractor, and BGV Plumbing handles permits so homeowners do not have to figure it out alone. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Collierville. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Collierville

    The cost of water heater installation in Collierville, TN can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical upgrades.     Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years.     A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.
