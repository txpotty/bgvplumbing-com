    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Collierville, TN

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Collierville, TN

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Collierville

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Collierville

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Plan on two to three hours.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Collierville, TN

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.
