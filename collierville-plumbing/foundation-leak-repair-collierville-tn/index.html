    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Collierville, TN

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can lay new tile or wood afterward.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Collierville

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. BGV Plumbing makes new crimp, expansion or solvent-weld joints so the line under your Collierville foundation is sound again.

    Water Line Reroute in Collierville

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Collierville, TN

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Collierville, TN. Tracer gas helps on tiny leaks.
