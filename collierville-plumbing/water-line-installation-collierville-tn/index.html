    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Collierville, TN

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Collierville

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a common choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Hot, humid summers and cool winters with frequent freeze and thaw cycles are the main climate stresses on plumbing in Tennessee. BGV Plumbing expertly installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Collierville. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a durable, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.
