    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Collierville, TN

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Collierville

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Collierville

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Humidity promotes condensation and corrosion on cold water lines and fittings in crawlspaces, which is worth checking during routine service. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.
