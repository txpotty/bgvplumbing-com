    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in Collierville

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most frequent reasons homeowners call us. We run a dedicated branch from the nearest adequate supply, install a shutoff valve behind the range and connect with a new approved flexible connector. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and ignition.

    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Collierville, TN

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Collierville

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are critical. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water efficiently.

    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Collierville, TN

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, practical option for gas line installation. BGV Plumbing is experienced with installing CSST, which can simplify routing and reduce the number of fittings compared to traditional black iron pipe. We make sure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as Tennessee code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Collierville home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in Collierville

    A simple indoor gas line for a range or dryer typically costs $350 to $1,000. Outdoor runs to a grill, fire pit or pool heater usually range from $1,000 to $3,500 depending on distance, trenching and line size.

    Materials like CSST and fittings carry manufacturer warranties, and we warranty our installation labor. We will review the specific terms with you before work starts.

    We use respected brands for gas line components, including Gastite and TracPipe for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe from reputable manufacturers. We also install reliable shutoff valves and fittings from brands like Mueller and Nibco for durability and safety.

    The most common sign of a gas leak is the smell of rotten eggs, which comes from an additive called mercaptan. Other signs include a hissing sound near a gas line, dead vegetation over an underground gas line or an unusual increase in your gas bill. If you suspect a leak, get out immediately and call 911 and your gas company.

    We use proven, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety. This includes manufacturers like Gastite and OmegaFlex for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe and fittings from reputable suppliers. All materials are chosen to meet or exceed local plumbing codes.

    Gas line installation is extremely dangerous and should only be performed by licensed professionals. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, explosions, fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It also voids warranties and often fails local inspections, so it is never a DIY project.

    Common materials include black iron pipe, which is very durable, and corrugated stainless steel tubing, which offers flexibility and can be easier to install. Copper tubing is sometimes used for smaller diameter lines but is less common for main lines. Our team selects the best material for your application and local codes.

    Most residential gas line installations for a single appliance can be completed in a few hours to one day. More complex projects, such as extending lines for outdoor kitchens or multiple appliances, might take one to two days. This timeline does not include permitting and inspections, which can add several days to the overall process.
