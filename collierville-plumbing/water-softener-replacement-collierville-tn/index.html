    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Collierville, TN

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Collierville

    A single-tank softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual-tank or premium systems run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a loop or a drain line can add a few hundred dollars, depending on how far the pipes have to go.

    Sizing comes from two numbers: your hardness in grains per gallon and how many gallons the household uses a day. Their product is the daily grain load. The resin bed gets sized for a regen every three to seven days.

    Salt-based softeners swap calcium and magnesium for sodium through ion exchange, so the water actually tests soft. A salt-free unit leaves the minerals in place and changes their form so they do not stick to surfaces as easily. Only one truly softens.
