    Pedestal Sink Installation in Clarksville, TN

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Clarksville

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Clarksville

    Vessel Sink Installation in Clarksville, TN
