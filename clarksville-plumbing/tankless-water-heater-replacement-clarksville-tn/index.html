    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Clarksville

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Rapid swings between mild and freezing weather in Clarksville can stress joints and fittings over time as pipes expand and contract. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Clarksville

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix.     Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Clarksville, TN

    If you are tired of running the tap waiting for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year.
