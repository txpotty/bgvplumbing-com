    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Clarksville

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, especially along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting choices. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Clarksville

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.
