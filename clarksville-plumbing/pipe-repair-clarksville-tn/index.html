    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. We cut CPVC carefully.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Clarksville, TN

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. It makes sense on sound pipe. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, so we lay out both options with real numbers and tell you which one we would pick for our own house. The decision is yours.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Clarksville

    A burst supply line can put several gallons a minute on the floor. Shut the main first. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. We locate the split, cut out the damaged section and replace it with matching pipe and fittings, then bring the system back up slowly. Nearby pipe gets a look too.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Clarksville

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Clarksville

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Clarksville

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Crawlspaces are the most common foundation here, though basements are more common in hilly areas where homes are built into slopes. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    Buried Line Repair in Clarksville

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. Water mains are common. We locate the leak without guessing, and either fix it at the mark or pull a new line through the old route. Drain lines get video first.
