    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Clarksville

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages.     Control Valve and Injector Service in Clarksville, TN

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Clarksville

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Clarksville, TN usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the system's condition and the services required.     A standard water softener maintenance service typically takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unforeseen issues are discovered during the inspection.

    Maintenance usually includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to confirm effectiveness.
