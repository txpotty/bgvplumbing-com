    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Clarksville

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Clarksville

    Residential ejector systems in Clarksville serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer.     Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Clarksville

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. The model comes from the pump curve for your lift, and most basement baths need a four-tenths or half horsepower unit that passes two-inch solids. Tall lifts need bigger pumps.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Clarksville, TN

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. That speeds up new installs, and their wide-angle float switches are less likely to hang up on the basin wall than older tethered floats, which is one of the most common reasons a pump fails to start.
