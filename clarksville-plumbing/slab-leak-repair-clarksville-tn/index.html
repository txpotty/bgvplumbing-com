    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Clarksville

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Drain lines under the slab leak too. Those leaks smell. A camera inspection shows the crack, sag or roots, and the repair might be a tunnel, a slab cut or a liner, depending on location and length. Health codes in Clarksville, TN cover this work.
