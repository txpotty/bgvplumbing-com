    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Clarksville

    Septic Pipe Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Karst ground with sinkholes is common in parts of Tennessee, and shifting soil above that rock can crack buried pipes over time. Roots and settling ground split joints and crush the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Drain Field Repair in Clarksville, TN

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Tee Repair in Clarksville

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.
