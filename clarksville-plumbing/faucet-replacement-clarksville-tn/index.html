    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Clarksville

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. A hose kit can fix that. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. If your valve is a Moen Posi-Temp or a Delta Monitor, a trim kit from the same brand bolts right onto it and gives you new handles, a new spout and a new showerhead. The valve has to match. If the valve itself is old, we talk about a valve upgrade.

    Budget-Friendly Faucet Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    Not every faucet swap needs a designer model. Mid-range Moen, Delta and Pfister faucets with brass bodies and ceramic cartridges often outlast expensive imports built around plastic. We will tell you where to spend. The price goes up for finish and styling, while the parts that matter stay about the same.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Clarksville

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. The hose wears, the spray button sticks, and after a few years the docking magnet or weight stops pulling the head back into place. We swap the whole faucet. The new hose weight gets clipped where the head docks cleanly, and both supplies are new braided lines.

    Crusty Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    Crusty lav faucets are the most common swap we do. Out comes the old faucet, the deck gets scraped clean, and the new faucet goes in with a matching pop-up drain. Figure an hour. We line up the handles before we leave.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle models use a ball and seats, while newer ones take a cartridge, which changes whether repair or replacement makes more sense. We tell you which you have. A sound body sometimes only needs parts.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Clarksville

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense bathroom faucets drop to 1.5 gallons per minute, and the aerated stream feels about the same in your hands. The savings show on the bill. We can swap a bath faucet for a WaterSense model in the same visit.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    A split hose bib often looks fine from outside. Before a freeze, homeowners here usually disconnect hoses, close crawlspace vents and let faucets drip on outside walls. Most splits sit inside the wall, either from trapped water or plain wear on a valve that has been cranked hard for years. The wall behind it gets checked. Where freezing can happen, we install a frost-free sillcock with a built-in vacuum breaker.
