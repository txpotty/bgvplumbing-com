    How We Replace Polybutylene in Clarksville, TN

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Clarksville

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. We replace those lines with PEX sized for the home and secure them properly to the frame. Exposed lines get checked. Most Tennessee winters bring a handful of hard freezes, and occasional arctic blasts can burst pipes in homes that are not ready for deep cold. We inspect heat tape and insulation on any exposed pipe and replace what is worn, so the new lines stay protected.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Clarksville, TN

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.
