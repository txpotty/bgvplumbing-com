    Anode Rod Replacement in Clarksville, TN

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a handful of years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems.
