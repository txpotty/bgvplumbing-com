    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Clarksville

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Clarksville, TN

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Clarksville

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Clarksville

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Clarksville

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Clarksville, TN

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Clarksville

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Clarksville, TN

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
