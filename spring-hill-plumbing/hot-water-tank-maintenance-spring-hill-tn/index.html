    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Spring Hill, TN

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Spring Hill

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Spring Hill, TN

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Spring Hill

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Spring Hill home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.
