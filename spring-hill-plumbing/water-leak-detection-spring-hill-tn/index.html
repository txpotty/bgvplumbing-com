    Water Meter Leak Testing in Spring Hill

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Spring Hill, TN

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Spring Hill

    Leaks in outdoor plumbing, irrigation systems or main water lines buried in your yard can lead to wasted water and landscaping damage. We use ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection to locate buried pipes and identify leaks without major digging. Our approach minimizes disruption to your lawn and garden, preserving your property's appearance. Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a critical concern for homes in Spring Hill, TN.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Spring Hill

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes with water softeners.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Spring Hill, TN

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause serious damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are skilled at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Humidity promotes condensation and corrosion on cold water lines and fittings in crawlspaces, which is worth checking during routine service.
