    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Spring Hill

    Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts. We pull a cartridge to check.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Spring Hill, TN

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Spring Hill, TN

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.
