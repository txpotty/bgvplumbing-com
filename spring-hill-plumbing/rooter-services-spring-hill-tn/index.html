    Hydro-Jetting Services in Spring Hill, TN

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. Grease, scale and fine roots that a cable rides over come off with the water. Cracked clay or thin cast iron can be hurt by that pressure, so we only jet a line we have seen and trust.
