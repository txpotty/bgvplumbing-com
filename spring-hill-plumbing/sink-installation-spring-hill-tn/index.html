    Undermount Sink Installation in Spring Hill

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Spring Hill

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
