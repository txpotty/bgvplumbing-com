    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in Spring Hill, TN

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer. For tubs, the cable goes in through the overflow plate rather than the drain, which protects the tub surface, and we clear the trap and the branch beyond it, then run the shower for a few minutes to confirm it drains at full speed with no gurgle.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Spring Hill

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? That points to the main. A blocked main affects every drain in the house, and the lowest fixture shows it first, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.

    Clogged Toilet Repair in Spring Hill, TN

    Toilets clog at the trapway, a tight bend inside the porcelain. We use a closet auger with a rubber guard on the bowl end. When a cleared toilet still backs up into the shower, the real blockage is farther down the line, and we move to a bigger machine at that point. Kids' toys come out too.

    Bathroom Drain Cleaning in Spring Hill, TN

    Hair mixed with soap scum makes a rope that wraps around the stopper linkage. That is where most bathroom clogs live. We pull the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable into the branch line, and if the vent is plugged and the drain still gurgles, we check that too before we pack up.

    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Spring Hill

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. It goes down warm and sets up like candle wax in the pipe. Bits of food and soap catch on that coating until the 1-1/2 inch line is down to the size of a pencil, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Spring Hill, TN

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Camera Inspection After Drain Cleaning in Spring Hill

    A camera shows what the cable felt. Self-leveling heads keep the picture upright. Older cast iron tends to show scale and a worn-through bottom, and clay pipe usually shows roots at the joints, and a locator marks the depth on the surface so a repair can be dug in the right spot.
