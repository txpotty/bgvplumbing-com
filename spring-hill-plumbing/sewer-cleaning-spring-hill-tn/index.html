    Drain cleaning usually refers to clearing clogs in smaller branch lines leading from individual fixtures, like sinks or tubs.     We strongly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer lines. These products are often ineffective against severe sewer clogs and can corrode pipes, especially older ones, causing more damage. They can also pose a health risk to you and our plumbers on the job.

    If our cleaning or camera inspection reveals damage like cracks, collapses, or severe root intrusion that cleaning alone cannot resolve, BGV Plumbing will give you a detailed assessment and options for sewer line repair or replacement.     While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY solutions, main sewer line blockages need professional tools and expertise. Trying to clear a sewer line without proper equipment can worsen the problem or damage your pipes.
