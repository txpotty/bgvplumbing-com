    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Spring Hill

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Spring Hill, TN

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. Self-regulating cable on a GFCI outlet, covered with insulation, keeps the pipe above freezing.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Spring Hill, TN

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a hose bib with the hose left on. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow devices sit fully exposed, so we drain or cover them before winter and repair split ones.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Spring Hill

    Drain lines can freeze too, mostly in crawlspaces and outside walls. A frozen drain backs up. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.

    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Spring Hill

    A faucet that trickles or goes dry on a cold morning usually means ice in the line feeding it. We trace the line back with a thermal camera to find the cold spot. We thaw it with the faucet open so melting water can escape.
