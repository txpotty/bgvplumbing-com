    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Spring Hill, TN

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Spring Hill, TN

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while repeat drywall, cabinet and floor repairs add up fast, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Spring Hill, TN

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Spring Hill

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Spring Hill, TN

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Spring Hill, TN

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Spring Hill

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Spring Hill

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Humidity promotes condensation and corrosion on cold water lines and fittings in crawlspaces, which is worth checking during routine service. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Spring Hill

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains. The size of the wall, how wet it gets, which method fits and how hard the wall is to get to all move the number. Access matters most. Nothing starts until you have the quote in writing.

    Sprinklers, nine times out of ten. Spray heads and drip lines aimed at the house soak the block every day, and soil sloped toward the wall or a short downspout adds to it, so the masonry stays wet and leaves mineral deposits as it dries. Sometimes adjusting a single sprinkler head solves it.

    Some handy homeowners do. Rolling on a liquid membrane is not hard, but seams, inside corners and the drain connection are where most leaks start, and a pan also needs a correct quarter-inch-per-foot slope plus a 24-hour flood test. A pro install lowers the odds of hidden damage.

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, covering crystalline products, elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Poured concrete takes crystalline coatings well, while block often needs an elastomeric membrane or a drainage board.

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out. Exterior waterproofing stops water before it reaches the wall, which means digging down to the footing and applying a membrane to the outside face. Outside costs more.

    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before we seal anything, and for larger growth, roughly anything over ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. Small patches we cut out and replace ourselves.

    Crack repairs take a few hours. Interior drain tile usually takes one to three days, and a full exterior dig around the whole house can run three to seven days or more depending on the weather and the depth of the footing. We give you a timeline at the first visit.

    Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years. The range is wide since soil movement, groundwater levels and how well it was installed all play a part, and a crack that opens later can let water around even a good membrane. Drain tile can silt up over time.
