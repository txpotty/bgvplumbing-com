    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Spring Hill

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup snags debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Spring Hill, TN

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are well suited for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Spring Hill.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Spring Hill, TN

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.
