    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Bristol

    Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most at risk during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. After replacing the bowl, we show you how to winterize it, which means shutting the stop, flushing and adding RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Bristol

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Brands We Match in Bristol

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Bristol, TN

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume.     Brand-Specific Bowl Replacement in Bristol, TN
