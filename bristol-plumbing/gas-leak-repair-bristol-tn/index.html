    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Bristol, TN

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Bristol, TN

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Every time somebody slides the stove out to mop, the connector takes another bend. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Bristol

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the pilot is lit.
