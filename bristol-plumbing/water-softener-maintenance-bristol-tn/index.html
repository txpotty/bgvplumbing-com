    Resin Rebedding in Bristol

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try.     Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Bristol, TN

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Bristol, TN

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.
