    InSinkErator Installation Done Right in Bristol, TN

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Bristol

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Bristol

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. A leak from the housing means the internal seal is gone. No one repairs that seal. We take down the old one, keep the dishwasher hose capped and set the new unit to the existing drain, trimming the tailpiece if the height changed.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Bristol, TN

    Old disposals are heavy and awkward to get rid of. We take them with us. That includes the old cord, the bent mounting ring if we replaced it and the corroded slip nuts, so nothing is left in a bag under your sink for you to deal with on trash day. Most replacements finish in one visit.

    Evolution Series Installation in Bristol

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Bristol, TN

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Bristol, TN

    Cost differences come mostly from the unit and the prep work. The cheapest job is a Badger 5 swap with a cord kit. At the top is an Evolution Excel going into a kitchen that needs a new flange and a lower trap arm, with most jobs in between. We put horsepower and warranty side by side.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Bristol, TN

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. Commercial units run 1 to 5 HP, usually on a dedicated circuit, and many need a water solenoid and a control panel mounted on the wall. We mount the unit and plumb the water feed, and your electrician handles the circuit. Health inspectors look at these. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we confirm that before the job starts.

    Badger Disposal Install in Bristol

    Most replacement disposals in area kitchens are a Badger. These 1/3 to 3/4 HP units handle everyday scraps and fit nearly any standard sink base. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.
