    Mineral buildup from harder water can wear out water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time.
