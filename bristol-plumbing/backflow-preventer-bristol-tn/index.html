    A failed test almost always comes down to rubber or debris. Check discs harden, relief valve diaphragms split and grit from the main settles on the seats, and any one of those is enough to fail.     Febco Backflow Preventer Experts in Bristol

    Febco assemblies are common on irrigation and commercial lines. We work on the 765 pressure vacuum breaker, the 850 double check and the 825Y and 860 reduced pressure assemblies. Each has its own quirks. Air inlet poppets fail on the 765, and relief diaphragms are the weak spot on the 825Y.

    Backflow Preventer Repair and Rebuild in Bristol, TN

    Worn check rubbers, grit under a seat and a relief valve that drips are the common failures. We isolate the assembly, take it apart and replace the worn parts with the manufacturer's rebuild kit, cleaning each seat before it goes back together. Then we retest. Rebuilding usually costs much less than replacing the whole assembly.

    Annual Backflow Testing in Bristol

    Most water providers require backflow assemblies to be tested at least once a year. We hook up a calibrated differential gauge and measure every check and the relief valve against the required numbers. Results go on the provider's form. A pass means you are set for a year.

    Freeze and Heat Protection for Backflow Preventers in Bristol, TN

    Above-ground backflow assemblies sit in the open through summer sun and winter cold.
