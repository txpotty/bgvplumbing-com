    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Bristol, TN

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in Bristol, TN

    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Replacing an Old Disposal in Bristol, TN

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected.     On new builds and remodels in Bristol, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.     Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Bristol, TN

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.
