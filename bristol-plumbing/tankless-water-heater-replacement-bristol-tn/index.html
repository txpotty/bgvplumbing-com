    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Bristol

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Rapid swings between mild and freezing weather in Bristol can stress joints and fittings over time as pipes expand and contract. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Bristol

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Bristol, TN typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any needed electrical or gas line upgrades. This price usually includes the unit itself and labor. We provide a written, upfront quote after a thorough assessment of your home.

    To extend the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is crucial, especially in areas with hard water. This usually involves annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, checking the air filter, and inspecting gas and water connections. BGV Plumbing offers maintenance plans to help keep your unit in top condition.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include frequent error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming common and costly, replacement is often the more economical choice.

    BGV Plumbing installs and replaces all major reputable brands of tankless water heaters. Our preferred brands include Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem, as they are known for their reliability, energy efficiency and long-term performance. We can also source and install other brands based on your preference or home requirements.

    To prepare for tankless water heater replacement, make sure the area around your existing unit is clear and accessible for our plumbers. If possible, clear a path to the installation site and keep pets or children safely out of the work area. We will handle draining the old unit and all other technical preparations.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement typically takes between 4 and 8 hours. This timeframe can vary based on whether we are replacing an existing tankless unit with a similar model or upgrading from a traditional tank water heater. A tank conversion may require more extensive changes to gas lines, electrical or venting.

    New tankless water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, which usually covers the heat exchanger for 10 to 15 years and parts for 3 to 5 years.
