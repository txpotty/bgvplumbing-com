    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Bristol, TN

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Bristol
