    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Bristol

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.
