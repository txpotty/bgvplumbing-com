    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Bristol

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Bristol, TN

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be applied to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Bristol, TN. This preserves your home's interior and minimizes renovation costs.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Bristol, TN

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Bristol, TN

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Tennessee homeowners.
