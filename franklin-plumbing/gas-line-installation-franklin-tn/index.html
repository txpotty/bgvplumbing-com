    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Franklin, TN

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in Franklin

    The cost for gas line installation in Franklin usually ranges from $500 to $2,500, depending on the length of the run, the pipe material, black iron or CSST, and the complexity of the installation. A simple hookup for a single appliance is usually on the lower end, while long runs or multiple connections cost more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Common materials include black iron pipe, which is very durable, and corrugated stainless steel tubing, which offers flexibility and can be faster to install. Copper tubing is sometimes used for smaller diameter lines but is less common for main lines. Our team selects the best material for your application and local codes.

    Materials like CSST and fittings carry manufacturer warranties, and we warranty our installation labor. We will review the specific terms with you before work starts.

    Yes, a permit is almost always required for any new gas line installation or significant change to an existing gas system. This ensures the work is performed to code and inspected for safety. We handle the permitting process in Franklin on your behalf to ensure compliance.

    The most common sign of a gas leak is the smell of rotten eggs, which comes from an additive called mercaptan. Other signs include a hissing sound near a gas line, dead vegetation over an underground gas line or an unexplained increase in your gas bill. If you suspect a leak, evacuate immediately and call 911 and your gas company.

    We use respected brands for gas line components, including Gastite and TracPipe for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe from reputable manufacturers. We also install reliable shutoff valves and fittings from brands like Mueller and Nibco for durability and safety.

    We mainly install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation. Both are safe and code-compliant when installed by licensed professionals, and the choice often depends on the application and property layout.
