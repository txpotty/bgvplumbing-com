    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Franklin, TN

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Franklin, TN homes are good candidates.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Franklin, TN

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Franklin

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Franklin homes with original plumbing are good candidates.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Franklin, TN

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.
