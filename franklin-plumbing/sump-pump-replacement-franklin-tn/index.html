    Sump Pump Types We Install in Franklin, TN

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Franklin

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Franklin, TN

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Backup Pump Options in Franklin, TN

    Strong storm systems can knock out power for hours, which is when a battery backup sump pump earns its keep in a basement home. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. Wells cannot run them.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Franklin, TN

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. Then we find out why it failed.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Franklin

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.
