    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Franklin

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Franklin

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.
