    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Franklin, TN: Style by Style

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Franklin, TN

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Franklin

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Franklin

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Franklin get their commercial sinks installed by BGV Plumbing. Hand sinks need tempered or hot and cold water. We install three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks with the indirect drains, floor sinks and grease trap connections the health inspector will look for.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Franklin, TN

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Franklin, TN

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Franklin, TN

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Franklin, TN

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. Power is a separate question. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Franklin, TN

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.
