    What Goes Into a Tankless Conversion in Franklin, TN

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Franklin

    Gas tankless units in Franklin are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Franklin, TN

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Mineral buildup from harder water can shorten the life of water heaters and leave scale on faucets, showerheads and dishwasher parts. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Franklin

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Franklin, TN

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Franklin

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Franklin, TN

    Businesses in Franklin, TN with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. Peak demand sets the rack size. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Franklin, TN

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Franklin, TN homes all year long. Rinnai brings range and parts support, Navien offers built-in recirc, Noritz makes compact condensing units for tight spots, and Rheem rounds out the list. Warranty terms vary. We explain the differences.
