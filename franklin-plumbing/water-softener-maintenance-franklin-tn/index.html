    Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have moderately hard water.     While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues.     Maintenance typically includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to confirm effectiveness.

    While maintenance itself doesn't usually extend the warranty period, most manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep your warranty valid. Neglecting maintenance can void your warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs. Always check your own warranty terms.
