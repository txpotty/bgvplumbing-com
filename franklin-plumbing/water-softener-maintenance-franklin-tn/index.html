    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Franklin

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They change the minerals into tiny crystals that are less likely to stick. The media needs replacing every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Franklin

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Franklin, TN

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. The bypass gets rebuilt or swapped out and check the drain line for kinks. Code requires an air gap at the drain.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Franklin, TN

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Franklin

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Franklin, TN

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Franklin, TN

    Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Morton and the store brands all use different valves, and parts for some of them only come through the dealer. We stock seal kits for the common ones. For a proprietary unit, you hear up front whether a part needs to be ordered and how long it usually takes.

    Resin Rebedding in Franklin

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.
