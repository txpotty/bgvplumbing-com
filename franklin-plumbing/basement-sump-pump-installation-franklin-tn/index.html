    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Franklin, TN

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Franklin

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. BGV Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Franklin home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season.     While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves complex plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing.
