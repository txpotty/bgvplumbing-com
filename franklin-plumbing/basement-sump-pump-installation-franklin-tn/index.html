    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Franklin

    Figure on roughly $800 to $2,000 for most basement sump pump installs in Franklin. The low end covers a pump swap in an existing pit, while a new pit cut through the slab, a long discharge run, or a battery backup pushes the total toward the top of that range. We quote after seeing it.

    For some homeowners, a same-model pump swap is a fair weekend job. Cutting a new pit or adding a backup is not, because those mistakes end in flooding, cracked slabs or electrical hazards, and fixing them costs more than paying for the install up front. Leave those to us.

    Look for a pump that runs nonstop, short cycles, or does not start when the pit fills. Corrosion on the housing and a float that hangs up are warning signs, and if your basement flooded even though you have a pump, the pump or its switch was not up to the job. Age matters too.

    Our installs carry a written warranty on both parts and labor. You will see the exact length in your service agreement, and the pump manufacturer adds its own coverage, which on a quality cast iron pump usually runs three to five years from the date of purchase. We register it for you.

    Once a year is enough for most pumps, ideally before the wet season. A tune-up covers the float, the pit, the check valve and the discharge, and we confirm the discharge outlet outside is open, since a clogged outlet makes the pump run hard against a blocked line. It takes under an hour.

    A primary pump does the everyday work. A secondary pump, often on a battery, waits in the same pit, and it kicks on if the primary fails, loses power, or gets overwhelmed by more water than it can move during a heavy storm. Each pump gets its own check valve.

    Mostly cast iron pumps from Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. All four have parts at local supply houses, which matters when a switch fails on a Sunday and you want the pump running again without waiting a week for an order to ship. Common models ride on our trucks.
