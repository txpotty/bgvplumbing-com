    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Franklin, TN

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Franklin, TN

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. Most of the remaining time goes into cleaning the flange and setting the new bowl on shims so it does not rock on a floor that has settled over the years. We haul the old one away.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Franklin, TN

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Franklin

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Franklin, TN

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Franklin

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Franklin

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Franklin

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Franklin, TN

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Start with the rough-in, since that decides which models fit at all. Then pick round or elongated. Elongated bowls are more comfortable but stick out about two inches farther, which can be a problem in a small half bath with the door swinging in. Check the MaP flush score too.
