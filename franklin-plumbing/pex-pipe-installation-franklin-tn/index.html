    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Franklin

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe.     PEX Main Water Line Installation in Franklin

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause major issues. We provide professional PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Franklin

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Franklin, TN

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Franklin, TN

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
