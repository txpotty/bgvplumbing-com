    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Franklin

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank usually costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installs often run $3,000 to $6,500, depending on how much gas line and venting work the house needs.

    Permits are common for water heater work. Water heater replacements, sewer line work and repiping usually require a permit, and inspection habits can vary from county to county. BGV Plumbing handles the permit and the inspection in Franklin.

    Two to four hours covers most standard tank installs. Tankless installs or jobs with major piping changes can take four to eight hours, mainly because of the venting and gas line work.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Tankless conversions and jobs that need new gas lines or wiring often take a full day. We give you a timeline first.

    An expansion tank absorbs the extra pressure created as water heats. Homes with a closed plumbing system are often required by code to have one, since it protects the heater and every pipe in the house.

    Manufacturer warranties on the tank and parts usually run anywhere from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model and the brand you choose. BGV Plumbing adds a labor warranty on our work. We review terms with you.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. Mistakes can cause gas leaks, CO exposure or electrical fires, and a DIY install can void the warranty. We do not recommend it.

    Rusty hot water, rumbling from the tank, water at the base and an age past ten years are the big ones. Short hot water often points to heavy sediment or a broken dip tube. We can inspect it.
