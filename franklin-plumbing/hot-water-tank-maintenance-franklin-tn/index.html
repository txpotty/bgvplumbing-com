    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Franklin

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Franklin

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Franklin, TN

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Franklin, TN.
