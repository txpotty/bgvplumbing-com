    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Franklin, TN

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Franklin, TN

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Franklin

    Backups happen at bad times. Nights and weekends included. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.

    Root Masses in Franklin Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Franklin

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Tree Root Removal in Franklin, TN Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Once inside, they grow fast. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Franklin, TN

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Franklin

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.
