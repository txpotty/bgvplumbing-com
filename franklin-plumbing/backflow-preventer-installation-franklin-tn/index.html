    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Franklin

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Franklin

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Franklin, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Residential Backflow Preventer Installation in Franklin

    For homeowners in Franklin, protecting your family's drinking water is the top priority. We install many types of backflow preventers, often required for irrigation systems, fire suppression lines or properties with alternative water sources. Our plumbers will assess your home's plumbing layout to recommend the most suitable device, meeting local health department regulations and protecting your potable water supply. We handle everything from small residential devices to bigger systems.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Franklin, TN

    For high-hazard applications in Franklin, TN where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. BGV Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Franklin, TN

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls and attics are the most vulnerable during a cold snap, especially where insulation is thin or missing. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.
