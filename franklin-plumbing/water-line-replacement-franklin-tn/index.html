    Service Line Replacement Choices in Franklin, TN

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Franklin

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Franklin

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. The zinc coating wears off and the steel rusts from the inside. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Pipe pulling works like this. Two pits get dug, one at each end. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Franklin, TN

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    Burst Main Line Response in Franklin, TN

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Franklin

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. A single meter may feed six or eight units. We map where each branch goes before cutting anything, coordinate with the utility for any work at the meter, and handle the permit, backflow test and inspection so the owner gets one point of contact. Paperwork included.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Franklin

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, and the launch and exit pits stay small. Heavy rock is the exception.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Franklin, TN

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.
