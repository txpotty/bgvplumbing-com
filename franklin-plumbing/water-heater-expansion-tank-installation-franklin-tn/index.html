    Tank Sizing and Placement in Franklin

    Correct sizing and placement are key to an effective water heater expansion tank in Franklin. Placement matters too, usually near the water heater on the cold water inlet line, with easy access for future maintenance or inspection.

    Standard Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Franklin

    Our standard installation service covers traditional tank-style water heaters, with the expansion tank correctly tied into your cold water supply line. We always test the system thoroughly after installation to confirm proper performance and compliance with local Franklin regulations.

    Replacing Old Expansion Tanks in Franklin

    Expansion tanks do not last forever, and an old, waterlogged or corroded tank offers no protection. If your existing tank in Franklin is showing signs of failure, such as a dripping relief valve or fluctuating water pressure, it is time for a new one. We carefully remove the old unit, dispose of it responsibly and install a new, correctly sized expansion tank. This timely replacement prevents costly damage to your water heater and plumbing system and restores proper pressure control.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.
