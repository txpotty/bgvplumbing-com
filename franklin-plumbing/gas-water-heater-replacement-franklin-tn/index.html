    Tankless Gas Conversion in Franklin

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. We help you pick.

    No Hot Water Calls in Franklin

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Franklin, TN

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Franklin

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Venting and gas work may be extra.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Franklin

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Franklin

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. We handle the sizing and the gas connection. Exhaust venting gets checked against their specs.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Franklin

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.
