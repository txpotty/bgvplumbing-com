    Bathroom Sink Installation in Franklin, TN

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Utility Sink Installation in Franklin

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. We set the tub on a sturdy stand or wall bracket, run a 1-1/2 inch trap so sand and lint do not stop it up, and add a faucet that a bucket fits under. Water hardness in Tennessee varies by source, and areas that draw from limestone aquifers or springs tend to have moderately hard water. A cleanout plug goes on the trap.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Franklin, TN

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Franklin, TN

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Franklin

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Franklin, TN

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Franklin

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. We install wall-hung and countertop units on carriers, add sensor or metering faucets, and set thermostatic mixing valves where code requires tempered water at public hand sinks. Vandal-resistant parts are available. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.
